# Chapter memory and continuity ledger: 027–036

## Scope and evidence method

This ledger records scene-grounded facts from the complete source text of Chapters 027–036, with the Chapter 026 closing scene and Chapter 037 opening checked as boundaries. The chapter file is the authority; existing chapter handoffs and the cumulative ledger were used as indexes, then checked against source scenes. Citations below use the numbered lines of `chapters/NNN-*.md` in this worktree. **CONFIRMED** means directly narrated or heard in the cited scene; **REPORTED** means a character says or sends it but the ledger does not certify it independently; **INFERRED** is a supported reading rather than explicit fact; **UNKNOWN** is not established by these sources. A mention, inference, or report is not a recovered memory.

The canon guardrails applied here are the Bible's fixed memory rules and family relationships: only Percy and Annabeth retain autobiographical memories of the erased future; Percy lacks first-person memory of the altered Atlantean childhood; Annabeth lacks first-person memory of the altered Grace childhood; Amphitrite must not learn Percy's memory predicament; Clarisse and Silena are Poseidon’s daughters and Percy’s half-sisters; Thalia, Annabeth and Jason are Grace siblings (FIC_CONTEXT.md §§2.1, 3.1–3.2, lines 61–84, 90–116). Ages and dates remain unknown unless stated in these source scenes; no calendar date is inferred from weekday, season, or chapter number.
