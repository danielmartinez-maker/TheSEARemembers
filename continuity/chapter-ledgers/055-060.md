## 056 — [Hades](../../chapters/056-hades.md#L1)

- **Court entry / witnessed events:** The four reach Hades's palace and register as living petitioners. An usher distinguishes Percy's welfare inquiry from a request to retrieve Sally and from the separate question of the Master Bolt. Percy recognizes them from camp stories; the source does not establish that the others had met them before (L101–111).
- **Hades's account vs. observations:** Hades denies taking Zeus's bolt, describes the cost a war would impose on the dead, and reports that his Helm of Darkness was stolen from his vault three weeks earlier. He says a god might have had vault access and that he does not know who crossed the threshold (L213–241). This is Hades's report, not something the children witnessed. Ch. 056 also says helm and bolt disappeared in the same turning of the moon (L227–233); exact theft dates and whether this means same day are not resolved. Hades's skeleton servant shows an empty cushion/clasp.
- **Nico's dream / Bianca's observations:** Nico reports a dream of a shadow/scythe figure speaking to a red god, with phrases about taking what shines and what hides. When Annabeth asks careful follow-ups, Nico says he cannot identify a face, a weapon carrier or a name; what he directly remembers is only the red figure and the repeated references (L243–311). Hades interprets the red god as Ares and the whisperer as someone seeking conflict; Grover reports a smell like old Titan dust/blood in woods, but says his senses cannot certify what Hades claims (L255–269). Bianca independently says the dead are restless, the pit is warmer than usual, and souls report a voice promising life; these are her reports, not the group's own experience (L267–275). Do not recast Nico's dream, Grover's smell or Hades's theory as eyewitness proof of the whole theft plan.
- **Hellfire / injuries / tactical limits:** Hades raises hellfire across the carpet. Percy senses water in palace conduits and raises a narrow flow, which flashes to steam; the water does not control the hellfire and Percy backs the group away. Annabeth tells him not to touch the Styx. Thalia shields Grover and confronts Hades about threatening her sister; Hades ends the flame by choice (L139–193). Percy has red, blistered palms afterward (L215). Annabeth's hand was already recovering from the earlier electrical injury and remains a concern; no second discharge into wet ground is attempted. Thalia's leather jacket smokes while she shields Grover; no new specific wound is confirmed here. The larger inference is that neither Percy nor Annabeth controls a god's element or has unlimited power.
- **The helm is found in Percy's pack:** While the court waits for transport records, Percy's backpack hums; he finds Hades's missing black iron helm inside, wrapped in red cloth he does not recognize. He and the others say they did not put it there and did not know it was in the pack. Bianca recognizes the cloth as similar to fabric used around Ares's spare weapons, then explicitly cautions that anyone could have taken it from the stores. Annabeth notes the wrapping is scratched but the bag is not; her written post-Arch inventory shows the outer pocket empty at that earlier check. She, Grover and Percy name the Lotus as a possible planting opportunity, but also state they cannot exclude an earlier courier. The group has not seen who planted it (L381–519). Hades takes possession of the returned helm and withholds judgment pending investigation; the source has a momentary variation in whether the hum stops at first touch or only after he sets it on the cushion (L469–527). Treat the helm as returned, but that sound detail is internally inconsistent.
- **Promises / obligations:** Hades demands a Styx oath to return the bolt before solstice, declare his innocence to Zeus and protect Bianca/Nico's location. Annabeth refuses to promise an outcome dependent on Ares; the party instead swears to protect the children's location and give an accurate account of the proceedings if they reach Olympus. They do not swear that they can force the bolt's return. Hades points them toward Ares at Santa Monica, framed as his belief/inference (“I think”), not independent confirmation (L561–609). Thalia receives Bianca's silver whistle with instructions to blow twice if lost; Percy touches Nico's Stygian sword hilt for luck but does not take the sword (L601–621).
- **Sally's reply / secret:** Hades reads the official entry: Sally has passed judgment, is in her assigned Fields, is not under punishment, and no transfer claim is pending. This is a written records response, not a meeting with Sally and not evidence that she heard Percy or knows about his erased-life memories. Hades refuses visitation and returns the stamped page with a silver notation and seal (L623–699). Percy admits he wants Sally back but does not claim entitlement or disclose the erased history to the court. Annabeth is the only person present who knows their erased-future memories; Thalia, Grover, Bianca, Nico and Hades receive no such disclosure. Percy later tells Annabeth he will share the written, explainable part with Clarisse and Silena, not the whole feeling (L843–847). Amphitrite remains uninformed.
- **Departure / physical state:** The group retains the response about Sally, route map, temporary wristbands, route copies and their prior papers; the Helm remains with Hades. Annabeth says her hand hurts enough she should not use lightning (L801–855). Grover retrieves his crackers and route map; Thalia keeps Bianca's whistle. They climb out with the whistle's help and the dead direct them; Annabeth stumbles and Percy catches her, then she returns the support. Percy and Annabeth promise to stop jumping in front alone and to work side by side; Thalia praises Annabeth and embraces her carefully. These promises follow their earlier quest vows and do not erase prior unilateral rescues (L723–799, L857–879). Exact surface-arrival time is UNKNOWN.
- **Open:** The Bolt remains missing at the end of the chapter; Ares's involvement is a strong working hypothesis, not yet proved to these witnesses. The helm thief, red cloth source, possible courier/planting route, details of the Lotus's role and exact time of the theft remain unknown. Sally's peace is partially answered by records; Percy has no visitation. Bianca/Nico's location is oath-protected.
