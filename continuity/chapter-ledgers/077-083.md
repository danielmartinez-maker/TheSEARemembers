This is a continuity index, not a prose rewrite or a declaration that every manuscript-wide issue is resolved.

## Reading conventions

- **[W] Witnessed/current:** the character is present and experiences the event directly.
- **[R] Reported:** a character learns it from a named speaker, letter, record, or other source; the character did not witness it.
- **[P] Private:** knowledge or intent limited to named characters or a private document/conversation.
- **[I] Inference:** interpretation rather than an established fact.
- **[U] Unknown:** the text does not establish it; do not fill it in by assumption.
- Prior-life memories belong only to Percy and Annabeth. Altered-childhood history is other characters' lived memory and does not become Percy or Annabeth's autobiographical memory merely because someone recounts it.
