# Chapter memory ledger: 001–048

## Scope and method

This is a source-anchored partial ledger, not a claim that Chapters 001–048 have all been audited. I personally read the complete scene text of Chapters 001–024; Chapters 025–048 remain unverified queue placeholders. For verified chapters, the chapter prose is the authority; chapter titles/filenames are locators. Do not use TODO entries as established character memory. Dates, elapsed chronology, locations and knowledge are marked unknown where the scenes do not establish them. The global ledger and handoff were consulted as indexes/context, not substitutes for scene reading. Adjacent chapter endings/openings were checked for the 001–024 boundary transitions; they do not establish extra off-page events.

Evidence labels: **CONFIRMED** = directly shown in personally read scene; **INFERRED** = supported interpretation, not explicit; **UNKNOWN/TODO** = not established here. Source pointers use chapter filenames plus numbered lines and/or short searchable prose anchors.

Percy conceals his memory gap but thanks her for answering about Sally (lines 39–72, 217–347, 626–797).
