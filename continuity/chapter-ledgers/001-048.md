# Chapter memory ledger: 001–048

## Scope and method

This is a source-anchored partial ledger, not a claim that Chapters 001–048 have all been audited. Chapters 001–024 have full-scene verification. Chapters 025–026 have source-section continuity passes with method limits stated in their entries. Chapters 027–036 have source-anchored full-scene continuity records, also collected in the detailed [027–036 ledger](027-036.md) with adjacent boundary checks. Chapters 037–040 have full-scene verification with adjacent boundary checks. Chapters 041–048 now have full-scene source-anchored records and Ch040/049 boundary checks in the detailed [041–048 ledger](041-048.md); that ledger is the current record for this range. For fully verified and section-screened chapters alike, the chapter prose is the authority; chapter titles/filenames are locators. Section screening must not be conflated with a line-by-line literary audit. Do not use TODO entries as established character memory. Dates, elapsed chronology, locations and knowledge are marked unknown where the scenes do not establish them. The global ledger and handoff were consulted as indexes/context, not substitutes for scene reading. Adjacent chapter endings/openings were checked for the 001–024 boundary transitions; they do not establish extra off-page events.

Evidence labels: **CONFIRMED** = directly shown in personally read scene; **INFERRED** = supported interpretation, not explicit; **UNKNOWN/TODO** = not established here. Source pointers use chapter filenames plus numbered lines and/or short searchable prose anchors.
