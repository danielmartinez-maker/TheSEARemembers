# Chapter memory ledger: 001–048

## Scope and method

This is a source-anchored partial ledger, not a claim that Chapters 001–048 have all been audited. I personally read the complete scene text of Chapters 001–012; Chapters 013–048 remain unverified queue placeholders. For verified chapters, the chapter prose is the authority; chapter titles/filenames are locators. Do not use TODO entries as established character memory. Dates, elapsed chronology, locations and knowledge are marked unknown where the scenes do not establish them. The global ledger and handoff were consulted as indexes/context, not substitutes for scene reading. Adjacent chapter endings/openings were checked for the 001–012 boundary transitions; they do not establish extra off-page events.

Evidence labels: **CONFIRMED** = directly shown in personally read scene; **INFERRED** = supported interpretation, not explicit; **UNKNOWN/TODO** = not established here. Source pointers use chapter filenames plus numbered lines and/or short searchable prose anchors.

## 001. The Weight of Salt and Sky — full scene verified

- **Source pointer:** `chapters/001-the-weight-of-salt-and-sky.md`, complete chapter text, lines 1–1,138.
- **POV/title:** Percy, third-person limited; title confirmed at source line 1.
- **Chronology/location:** Percy wakes twelve years old with seventeen-year-old prior-life memories. Departure is planned “in a week,” followed by several days of preparation; he leaves Atlantis in the morning and reaches camp that day. Exact calendar date and total elapsed time are **UNKNOWN**. Atlantis (palace, study, anemone garden, chamber/balcony), open water, Long Island shore, Half-Blood Hill, Camp Half-Blood, Athena cabin, Big House, dining pavilion, canoe lake and cabins are shown. Anchors: lines 1–3, 73–74, 156, 176, 564, 626, 800–802, 992, 1040, 1068, 1128.
- **Percy’s firsthand events/new memories:** Wakes disoriented, remembers Sally and his prior life with Annabeth, and conceals the discrepancy from Amphitrite. Poseidon embraces him, discusses camp arrangements, and privately tells Percy about an oath to guard ancient sacred-site locations and a recent disturbance (lines 89–154). Percy has a tense sword-practice exchange with Triton; he plays Tessera and talks about Sally with Amphitrite, who says Sally died when Percy was two and describes Sally through Poseidon’s stories (lines 156–347). Percy promises palace children he will return (lines 564–623), talks privately with Triton about the surface (lines 600–623), leaves with Poseidon and Amphitrite by hippocamp-drawn chariot, and promises Amphitrite to call daily and return if something is wrong (lines 626–797). Grover meets him as a new camper and escorts him to camp; Percy asks Grover to take him to Annabeth (lines 798–847). Percy and Annabeth privately confirm they both remember their former life and relationship; Annabeth describes her current identity as Thalia’s sister and the altered family she cannot remember autobiographically. They agree to be careful and to work things out; Percy takes Annabeth’s hand, she interlaces their fingers, and he promises to stay and help (lines 848–990). Grover notices Percy’s intense recognition of Annabeth and asks about it; Percy gives a limited explanation and Grover does not press (lines 992–1037). Chiron welcomes Percy, notes his prior sword training, offers a private talk, and directs him to his sisters (lines 1024–1037). At dinner Percy meets Clarisse, who calls herself a Poseidon-cabin camper and warns him not to drown in the canoe lake; Silena is not yet personally met in this chapter (lines 1040–1066). Percy and Annabeth talk at the canoe lake about their changed family histories and grief; they agree to talk again tomorrow. Percy ends the day in Cabin Three, aware Clarisse and another sister occupy the cabin, but the latter is not introduced on-page (lines 1068–1137).
- **Knowledge and secrets:** Percy and Annabeth explicitly tell each other about their remembered prior life and the change (lines 848–910); their shared private disclosure is known to those two. At this point no family member has been told. Grover hears an evasive, bounded explanation of Percy’s recognition, not the timeline secret (lines 992–1023). Chiron offers a private conversation, but no secret disclosure to him occurs. **Amphitrite is not told and does not discover the predicament**: Percy calls his state a weird dream and presents his anxiety as camp nerves; she interprets his behavior through leaving home (lines 39–72, 371–434). Percy hears from Amphitrite that Sally died when he was two and receives her account of Sally secondhand; he does not witness the altered-history events she describes (lines 217–275). Percy alone hears Poseidon’s ancient-sites confidence (lines 122–154); Amphitrite knows of the oath but not its locations, as Poseidon says.
- **Relationship changes / forms of address:** Percy and Amphitrite begin with established altered-childhood closeness on her side; she calls him *agapi mou*, kisses his forehead, comforts him and hugs him goodbye. Percy conceals his memory gap but thanks her for answering about Sally (lines 39–72, 217–347, 626–797). Percy and Poseidon share a warm father-son bond; Percy is still learning the altered household, and Poseidon notices his unusual distance (lines 89–154). Percy and Triton are brothers with teasing familiarity; Triton challenges Percy's claim to experience and shares his own surface story (lines 156–174, 600–623). Percy and Annabeth confirm established romance in their remembered life and create a new present-life memory of mutual recognition, handholding, and a promise to remain together (lines 848–990). Annabeth and Thalia have altered-history sisterhood known firsthand to Thalia; Annabeth says she cannot recall it and calls Thalia a stranger who knows her too well. Thalia displays practiced care, fixes Annabeth’s hair, notices her skipped lunch, and is suspicious of Percy; no reconciliation or trust is established (lines 911–970). Percy and Grover meet for the first time in this current history; Grover serves as escort and notices Percy’s unusual familiarity with Annabeth (lines 798–847, 992–1023). Percy and Clarisse are newly introduced half-siblings; she is blunt and competitive, and no trust or commitment is established (lines 1052–1066).
- **Carry-forward objects, obligations, injuries:** Percy leaves Atlantis with travel luggage; he and Amphitrite argue over packing, with two pairs of sandals retained on her terms (lines 176–347, 427–463). A portrait of Sally is in Poseidon’s study; Amphitrite says Poseidon has photographs, a box of Sally’s letters, and possibly her scarf, but Percy does not receive or inspect these items (lines 201–275). The chapter establishes Percy’s daily-call promise and his return-if-wrong promise to Amphitrite, plus his promise to the palace children to return (lines 564–623, 626–797). No injury or gift is established. Annabeth and Percy’s “tomorrow” conversation is an immediate commitment to continue comparing what happened (lines 1080–1125).
- **Unresolved:** Cause/architect of the timeline change, Annabeth’s full altered-history adjustment, whether the palace children will see Percy again, and what Poseidon’s disturbance concerns all remain unresolved. Percy’s future access to Sally’s photographs/letters/scarf is only proposed, not fulfilled. Clarisse says Percy has another sister; Silena has not yet appeared to him on-page.
