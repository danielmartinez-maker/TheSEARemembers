## Evidence labels

- **WITNESSED**: shown in the current-timeline scene; this labels only characters present or directly participating.
- **REPORTED**: attributed to a speaker, letter, or record; recipients did not witness the underlying event unless noted.
- **PRIVATE**: a character’s own thought, memory, or feeling, not automatically shared.
- **INFERRED**: a cautious reading supported by the text, not a settled fact.
- **UNKNOWN / OPEN**: the chapters do not establish the answer. Do not fill these gaps from implication alone.
