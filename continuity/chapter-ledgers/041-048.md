# Chapter continuity ledger: 041–048

## Scope and method

This is a source-anchored scene continuity record for Chapters 041–048, with the [Chapter 040 close](../../chapters/040-winter.md#L828) and [Chapter 049 opening](../../chapters/049-the-quest.md#L3) checked as boundaries. All eight assigned chapter files were read in full. `FIC_CONTEXT.md` and the applicable canon-bible rules were checked; earlier chapter-ledger entries, chapter audits and revision handoffs were used as indexes, not substitutes for the chapter text. The story chapters were not edited.

**Evidence labels:** **CONFIRMED** means directly shown in the cited current-timeline scene. **REPORTED** means a character states or conveys information they did not personally witness. **INFERRED** means a plausible reading, not an established fact. **UNKNOWN** means the prose does not establish it. The source chapter is authoritative when a summary or handoff differs.

## Locked fact sheet used for this span

Only Percy and Annabeth retain autobiographical memories of the seventeen-year-old prior life; other characters do not gain those memories from dreams, rumors or the children’s behavior. These chapters do not disclose the full timeline secret to Amphitrite or establish that she knows Percy’s autobiographical gap. See `FIC_CONTEXT.md` §§2.1–2.2 and the canon bible §§2.1–2.2.

Thalia and Annabeth are Zeus’s daughters and biological sisters; Thalia is the older sister who raised Annabeth. Percy and Annabeth remain an established romantic couple, not siblings. See `FIC_CONTEXT.md` §3.

A sibling’s account of a childhood event is that sibling’s firsthand memory and the other child’s present-day received report. See `FIC_CONTEXT.md` §2.2.

Treat the explicit age wording as source evidence, not proof of an on-page birthday.
