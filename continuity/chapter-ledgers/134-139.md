## Knowledge and memory provenance

- **Locked boundary:** only Percy and Annabeth retain firsthand erased-future memories. They remain established partners; no other character’s reported knowledge becomes firsthand memory. Percy does not acquire autobiographical recollection of rewritten childhood from what relatives tell him. Amphitrite remains unaware of his memory predicament. The chapters in this span do not authorize changing these rules (bible §2.1; see [134 L609–619](../../chapters/134-the-secret-passage.md#L609), [136 L795–805](../../chapters/136-percy-s-birthday.md#L795), [137 L866–868](../../chapters/137-fourteen.md#L866)).

The pipe identity remains uncertain, and his water sense is not treated as a definitive maintenance reading ([134 L223–269](../../chapters/134-the-secret-passage.md#L223), [L816–888](../../chapters/134-the-secret-passage.md#L816), [139 L203–217](../../chapters/139-storm-season.md#L203)).

Her skill growth is observable practice, not newly recovered childhood memory ([135 L551–619](../../chapters/135-the-garden.md#L551), [136 L785–805](../../chapters/136-percy-s-birthday.md#L785), [139 L405–445](../../chapters/139-storm-season.md#L405)).
