## Evidence labels and canon frame

- **WITNESSED:** Directly shown in the current-timeline scene; only participants/witnesses gain firsthand knowledge.
- **REPORTED:** A character retells or reads information; recipients do not thereby witness its source event.
- **PRIVATE:** Thought, remembered prior life, feeling, or object kept undisclosed; not shared by implication.
- **INFERRED:** Cautious interpretation supported by scene evidence.
- **UNKNOWN / OPEN:** Not established; preserve as unknown.
