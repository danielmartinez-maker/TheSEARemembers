# Character Memory Ledger

**Purpose:** Keep durable, character-specific knowledge and relationship history available across chapter gaps. This is an operating record, not a change to canon.

**Coverage:** This first seed checks selected scenes in Chapters 1, 11, and 12, plus the Chapters 111–113 continuity handoff. It is not a retrospective audit of all 193 chapters. An absent entry never means a character forgot or never experienced something. Before using a callback, search the actual earlier chapter and add the verified event.

## Rules that apply to everyone

- Memory belongs to the character who experienced or learned the event. Record who saw or heard it, who was told, and what each person can reasonably infer. Do not transfer private knowledge through narration or an off-page conversation.
- Current-life memories persist unless the story establishes forgetting, correction, deception, or a change in understanding. Time skips and chapter gaps are not memory loss.
- Preserve consequential interactions: meaningful forms of address, routines, care, conflict, apologies, promises, gifts, and disclosures. A character can behave differently later, but the story needs to carry the event forward or establish why their response changed.
- Emotional states are not frozen. Record the last evidenced state and any intervening change; do not invent feelings or hold a character in the same mood forever.
- Keep the special time-rewrite boundary separate from ordinary memory. Percy and Annabeth retain memories of the older life. Everyone's experiences and knowledge gained in the altered life still accumulate normally.
- Mark each entry **confirmed**, **inferred**, or **unknown**. Keep sent, received, read, and answered as separate states.

## Memory update template

Add a brief entry to the chapter handoff and to the relevant character or relationship section here whenever a chapter creates a durable change.

```yaml
- chapter: 000
  source_scene: ""
  character: ""
  event_or_fact: ""
  basis: witnessed | told_by_character | inferred
  shared_with: []
  certainty: confirmed | inferred | unknown
  current_status: active | resolved | corrected | superseded | unknown
  carry_forward: ""
```

For a promise, add who made it, to whom, what would fulfill it, and whether it remains open. For an emotional change, record its trigger and last evidenced state. Do not overwrite the original event; append the later resolution or correction.

### Direct-address check

**Chapter 1 does not establish Percy calling Amphitrite “Mom.”** When he asks about “my mom,” he means Sally; he answers “Sally” when Amphitrite asks “Which one?” He later addresses Amphitrite by name. **[Confirmed: Chapter 1, anemone-garden conversation.]**

The “Mom in Chapter Y, stranger in Chapter Y+10” scenario is a **conditional continuity test**, not an established canon event. If Percy directly calls Amphitrite “Mom” and she hears him, record that shared event and its context for both characters. Ten chapters later, neither should behave as if they have just met. Percy may be awkward, conflicted, formal, or distant, but the scene must preserve his awareness of their history and account for the change. A single address does not erase his grief for Sally, make Amphitrite aware of the time rewrite, or automatically settle the relationship. If he only thinks the word or she does not hear it, track that distinction.

## Locked knowledge boundaries to carry forward

- **Percy and Annabeth:** They remember the older life, including their relationship. They do not have complete first-person memories of their altered-history childhoods. **[Confirmed: FIC_CONTEXT.md §2.1.]**
- **Amphitrite:** She knows Sally died when Percy was two in the altered history and that she helped raise him. She does not know Percy remembers another life or lacks autobiographical access to his Atlantean childhood. Do not reveal that to her without explicit author authorization. **[Confirmed: FIC_CONTEXT.md §2.1.]**
- **Thalia:** She remembers her altered-history childhood with Annabeth. Any older-life information Percy shares with her is reported information, not her own memory. The extent of her knowledge is limited to what is actually said in the cited scenes. **[Confirmed: FIC_CONTEXT.md §§2.1, 3.2; Chapters 11–12.]**
- **Other characters:** Do not assume they remember the erased future. Do record everything they experience or are told in the present story, using the same template.

## Before and after a chapter

**Before drafting or revising:**

1. List every returning character who appears or is discussed.
2. Check this ledger, the previous chapter's actual ending, the relevant handoff, and the earlier source scenes for callbacks. Do this even when the callback is ten or more chapters old.
3. Write down each returning character's relevant knowledge, relationship history, open promises, and last evidenced emotional state. If a source is missing, mark it unknown and inspect the chapter.

**After drafting or revising:**

1. Add durable events and disclosures for every affected person, including off-POV listeners and witnesses.
2. Update whether a promise was fulfilled, a letter was received, a fact was corrected, or a relationship changed only when the text establishes that status.
3. Add the next-appearance reminder to the handoff. If the next scene intentionally changes the prior pattern, record the cause.
4. If an older chapter is revised, check later appearances that depend on it; do not silently repair unrelated chapters.
