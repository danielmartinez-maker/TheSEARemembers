# Per-chapter character memory handoff

Use before drafting or revising **every** chapter. The facts belong to the character who experienced or learned them, not to an omniscient shared brain. Read the actual closing scene of the preceding chapter and any original scene being recalled.

## Pre-scene verification

1. List everyone physically present. For each important pair, check the earliest meeting, the most recent shared scene, and any major trust/romance/family milestone between them.
2. Confirm current terms of address, family roles, relationship status, distance since last visit, and any unresolved argument or promise. Earlier closeness does not vanish because the plot changes location.
3. Separate firsthand memories from letters, hearsay, stories, mistaken inference, and secrets. A person who was absent is not entitled to the exact dialogue.
4. For Percy and Annabeth, distinguish **original-life memories**, **new current-chapter memories**, and **altered-childhood stories they were told**. Never let Amphitrite deduce or learn their timeline secret unless the author explicitly changes that locked rule.
5. After the scene, record what changed, who witnessed it, and what the next chapter must carry forward.
