# Frank Zhang: cumulative memory

**Entry coverage:** Source-verified appearances in Chapters 083 and 093 only; intervening chapters are not audited here.

## Verified cumulative timeline (Chapters 001–193)

- **Chapter 066 — reported, not firsthand to the Greeks:** In a letter to Thalia, Jason introduces Hazel and Frank as his close friends, describes Hazel's age and Pluto parentage and Frank's ability to take animal forms, and asks whether Frank and Percy might get along. Thalia and the Greek family can remember Jason's written description; none of them witnessed Frank's Roman life from the letter ([Ch066](../../chapters/066-jason-s-letter.md#L157), [Ch066](../../chapters/066-jason-s-letter.md#L177)).
- **Chapter 073 — direct Camp Half-Blood visit:** Frank contributes hinged, collapsible set pieces and helps build and operate the play's fountain and breakaway panel. He troubleshoots the water valve, times the final fountain effect, and accepts compliments after the show ([Ch073](../../chapters/073-the-play.md#L121), [Ch073](../../chapters/073-the-play.md#L431), [Ch073](../../chapters/073-the-play.md#L567), [Ch073](../../chapters/073-the-play.md#L931), [Ch073](../../chapters/073-the-play.md#L947)).
- **Chapter 083 — spring exchange:** Frank welcomes the Greek visitors, participates in the visit and war game, and shares the fireworks hill with Hazel, Reyna and the visiting family. He gives Percy a small clay trireme; he packs food for the departing group and offers to ask the Fifth Cohort about an engineer for the proposed shared-library work ([Ch083](../../chapters/083-spring-break-visit.md#L219), [Ch083](../../chapters/083-spring-break-visit.md#L361), [Ch083](../../chapters/083-spring-break-visit.md#L429), [Ch083](../../chapters/083-spring-break-visit.md#L849), [Ch083](../../chapters/083-spring-break-visit.md#L861)).
- **Chapter 093 — bounded visit:** Frank lends Percy a clean Camp Jupiter shirt for the night, cheers during the film, steadies the screen when asked, and comforts Ella with a napkin. Percy returns the shirt at first bell ([Ch093](../../chapters/093-movie-night.md#L262), [Ch093](../../chapters/093-movie-night.md#L307), [Ch093](../../chapters/093-movie-night.md#L523), [Ch093](../../chapters/093-movie-night.md#L939)).
- **Relationship and unknowns:** Jason's letter calls Frank one of his best friends; direct Camp scenes establish Frank and Hazel's shared presence and comfortable closeness but do not state a formal relationship status. Frank's later contact with the Greek campers and the fate of the proposed library project are unknown in the reviewed scenes. He gains no private Percy/Annabeth memory from these visits.

**Entry coverage:** Source-verified reported contact in Chapter 066 and firsthand milestones in Chapters 073, 083 and 093. Incidental name mentions are not separate contact records; later knowledge is unknown.
