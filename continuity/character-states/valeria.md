# Valeria — Roman contact / cumulative memory

**Ch019:** Roman aide helping supervise Jason's Camp Jupiter Iris call. She is witness to whatever Jason/Greek campers said on live call and assists approved drawing transfer. She is NOT a witness to private Percy/Annabeth original-life conversations, and an accepted physical letter for Roman processing does not prove it reached Jason.

**Ch021–022:** Roman authorities separately APPROVE Jason's seven-day supervised Greek-camp visit. Valeria's personal role in authorizing or escorting the visit is not established unless a later scene names her; do not infer.
