# Barnacle — cumulative event memory

**Ch014 FIRSTHAND:** Hippocamp courier who brings Triton's/Amphitrite's sealed undersea package to Camp Half-Blood for Percy. Percy, Clarisse and Silena see delivery; Silena checks manifest. The three bracelets, jam, blanket, training manual, conch and Poseidon letter are RECEIVED. Barnacle is not evidence that earlier personal letters were received in Atlantis or that Jason mail reached Rome.

## Verified cumulative timeline (Chapters 001–193)

- **Chapter 014 — confirmed delivery:** Barnacle is the named hippocamp courier who arrives at the beach with the undersea package for Percy. Percy greets him familiarly; Barnacle is excited, accepts sugar-kelp treats, and carries the delivered package, whose contents the recipients unpack and divide ([Ch014](../../chapters/014-letters-from-under-the-sea.md#L275), [Ch014](../../chapters/014-letters-from-under-the-sea.md#L289), [Ch014](../../chapters/014-letters-from-under-the-sea.md#L291), [Ch014](../../chapters/014-letters-from-under-the-sea.md#L317)). At the end of the day, the family sends him off with more kelp and a thank-you note tied to his mane ([Ch014](../../chapters/014-letters-from-under-the-sea.md#L781)).
- **Knowledge and limits:** The scene confirms this one delivery and his friendly familiarity with Percy. Other uses of “Barnacle” later are nicknames or jokes, not evidence that this courier returned, carried another message, or heard private conversations. His later whereabouts and knowledge are unknown.

**Entry coverage:** One source-verified courier appearance in Chapter 014. Later nickname mentions are not treated as contact.
