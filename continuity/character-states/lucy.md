# Lucy — cumulative character memory

**First verified source:** [Chapter 072](../../chapters/072-new-kids.md), one of three new year-round arrivals in April. Lucy is about eleven, wears red braids, and arrives with light-up sneakers her mother gave her. Her surname, divine parent, assigned cabin, counselor, and earlier history are not established.

## Verified cumulative timeline (Chapters 072–090)

- **Chapter 072 — arrival and first-day choices:** Lucy is car-sick on arrival and says she misses her mother. She chooses the bathrooms on the optional tour; after the cabin stop, Annabeth points out the path and Lucy walks it with her to see the entrance sign. She asks about the nightlight arrangement and asks Annabeth to wait outside her counselor meeting and tell her before leaving. Annabeth agrees; the meeting's outcome and whether Lucy receives a nightlight are not shown ([arrival and choices](../../chapters/072-new-kids.md#L59), [bathroom route](../../chapters/072-new-kids.md#L209), [nightlight and counselor arrangements](../../chapters/072-new-kids.md#L655)). At the arena she demonstrates the open-hand/“hold” stop signal and chooses to watch rather than handle a sword ([signal](../../chapters/072-new-kids.md#L281), [watching from the fence](../../chapters/072-new-kids.md#L315)). The group later offers a brief, optional view of the infirmary; Lucy looks from the entrance ([infirmary orientation](../../chapters/072-new-kids.md#L685)). Her light-up shoes get wet near the tide; she says her mother gave them and does not consent to anyone opening them. The group only proposes a later check; no inspection or diagnosis occurs that day ([shoes in the tide](../../chapters/072-new-kids.md#L797), [mother-gifted shoes](../../chapters/072-new-kids.md#L823)). Lucy chooses to stay **that night only**; the next day remains undecided ([closing choice](../../chapters/072-new-kids.md#L1009)).
- **Chapter 073 — camp play:** Lucy attends with a counselor, sits beside Milo, chooses plain camp shoes, and giggles at Percy when he bows. This does not establish the outcome of her counselor meeting or a check of the light-up shoes ([audience scene](../../chapters/073-the-play.md#L881)).
- **Chapter 075 — Zoe's story:** Lucy stays for the story after Bea leaves with her counselor and asks Zoe about home and why she left. Lucy says her mother calls her “Loops” in notes; that is Lucy's report of a familiar nickname, not evidence that a note was sent to camp or that current contact occurred. Lucy supports Milo as he considers writing to his brother, shows him her pegasus drawing, and keeps it when he chooses to draw his own ([story and family questions](../../chapters/075-zoe-s-story.md#L31), [nickname and support for Milo](../../chapters/075-zoe-s-story.md#L597), [drawing boundary](../../chapters/075-zoe-s-story.md#L657)).
- **Chapter 077 — beach day:** Lucy helps direct newcomers to a safe place for bags and shoes, suggests an access ramp for Annabeth's sandcastle, and helps shape and test it. Later she draws a starfish she calls “Orange Captain” for an Apollo camper's injured friend. These are witnessed acts of participation and care, not an ongoing assignment or cabin role ([bag area](../../chapters/077-beach-day-atlantis-style.md#L182), [ramp](../../chapters/077-beach-day-atlantis-style.md#L408), [drawing](../../chapters/077-beach-day-atlantis-style.md#L876)).
- **Chapter 080 — dance:** A speaker pop and crowd distress her. Lucy asks to go outside and leaves with Milo, Bea, and their counselor by the quiet porch route. When the system is unplugged and the music becomes acoustic, she chooses to return for the final group song and practices a step. She can leave and return at her own pace ([exit](../../chapters/080-the-second-dance.md#L809), [return](../../chapters/080-the-second-dance.md#L1007)).
- **Chapter 081 — retrospective reference:** Percy recalls Lucy's access-ramp suggestion; the reference adds no new Lucy action or knowledge ([Chapter 081](../../chapters/081-home.md#L613)).
- **Chapter 085 — route test, first Tuesday in June:** Lucy finishes shoe work before testing the newcomer route with her counselor. One light is not blinking, so she postpones repair. Lucy recalls falling at the creek bridge during her first week and says she was embarrassed to tell anyone. During the route test she identifies unclear signs and a sign placed too high, learns where the nighttime infirmary bell is and how to get guidance from Will, and receives credit on the revised map. A later scene shows her checking signs with her counselor. The chapter does not say the shoe was repaired or the bridge hazard fully fixed ([route and shoe](../../chapters/085-spring.md#L181), [fall disclosure](../../chapters/085-spring.md#L213), [revised map](../../chapters/085-spring.md#L223), [later sign check](../../chapters/085-spring.md#L679)).
- **Chapter 089 — rescheduled Valentine event:** Lucy draws a pegasus on a thank-you note for a stable volunteer who helped repair one of her light-up shoes and asks for private delivery. This confirms a repair to one shoe by this point; it does not establish an electrical or saltwater safety inspection of both shoes ([note and repair](../../chapters/089-belated-valentines-camp-style.md#L143)).
- **Chapter 090 — storm response:** Lucy helps Bea walk behind the counselor during evacuation ([evacuation](../../chapters/090-storm-and-sea.md#L151)).

## Relationship and knowledge boundaries

- Lucy identifies her mother as the source of her light-up shoes, says she misses her, and reports that her mother uses the nickname “Loops.” No other family member is named. The text does not establish camp-era contact with her mother.
- Percy and Annabeth are her new friends and guides, not guardians. Annabeth agrees to wait outside Lucy's counselor meeting and tell Lucy before leaving; fulfillment or outcome is not recorded. The children's choice to remain at camp in Chapter 072 is for one night only.
- Lucy directly hears that Percy, Clarisse, and Silena are Poseidon's children and that Thalia and Annabeth are Zeus's daughters/sisters. She also learns that Percy and Annabeth are dating. She is not shown learning or remembering their private erased-future memories.
- No surname, divine parentage, cabin placement, named counselor, shoe inspection, confirmed nightlight delivery, or later-day commitment is established. Do not merge her with another camper or infer an unshown conversation from her presence in a group scene.
