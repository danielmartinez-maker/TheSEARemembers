## Verified cumulative timeline (Chapters 001–193)

- **Chapter 045 — courtship and accepted song:** Grover gives Juniper a private note; she reads it, responds positively, and later joins him under her favorite maple for the song he wrote for her. They hold hands, share a strawberry, and leave the scene together; Grover does not make the performance public against her wishes ([Ch045](../../chapters/045-grover-s-crush.md#L418), [Ch045](../../chapters/045-grover-s-crush.md#L450), [Ch045](../../chapters/045-grover-s-crush.md#L474), [Ch045](../../chapters/045-grover-s-crush.md#L768), [Ch045](../../chapters/045-grover-s-crush.md#L1023), [Ch045](../../chapters/045-grover-s-crush.md#L1039)).
- **Chapters 049 and 055 — message plan, dispatch unknown:** Before the quest, Grover writes that he wants to tell Juniper before leaving, but the chapter does not show the note sent. Later he asks Martin to relay a message if the Underworld blocks contact; delivery is not confirmed ([Ch049](../../chapters/049-the-quest.md#L493), [Ch055](../../chapters/055-underworld-gates.md#L77)).
- **Chapter 060 — shared serviceberry task:** Juniper and Grover plant the sapling for his petrified Uncle Ferdinand's possible recovery, put it at a site approved by the groundskeeper, and create a watering rota with younger satyrs and a named groundskeeper. This is a care plan, not evidence that Ferdinand has recovered ([Ch060](../../chapters/060-summer-s-end.md#L159), [Ch060](../../chapters/060-summer-s-end.md#L163), [Ch060](../../chapters/060-summer-s-end.md#L181), [Ch060](../../chapters/060-summer-s-end.md#L197), [Ch060](../../chapters/060-summer-s-end.md#L203)).
- **Chapters 080–082 — relationship clarified during absence/return:** After Juniper returns from an extended absence, Grover, Ella and Juniper speak directly about what each relationship means. Juniper accepts a group invitation as Grover's friend but says she does not want to be treated as an escort or left outside; she and Ella choose to walk together for some of the dance. Later Juniper attends as a friend and partner, and Grover thanks her. Their relationship remains established and distinct from Ella's chosen date for that evening ([Ch080](../../chapters/080-the-second-dance.md#L545), [Ch080](../../chapters/080-the-second-dance.md#L587), [Ch080](../../chapters/080-the-second-dance.md#L619), [Ch080](../../chapters/080-the-second-dance.md#L629), [Ch080](../../chapters/080-the-second-dance.md#L703), [Ch080](../../chapters/080-the-second-dance.md#L757)). When Grover leaves for his Searcher route, Juniper cries and gives him seventeen pinecones; the letter later reports that she was busy as a tree and had not answered for part of an afternoon, not that she had abandoned him ([Ch081](../../chapters/081-home.md#L201), [Ch082](../../chapters/082-letters-may.md#L308), [Ch082](../../chapters/082-letters-may.md#L604)).
- **Chapters 093, 097 and 100 — established partnership:** Chapter 093 calls Juniper Grover's girlfriend; she attends the movie night, jokes about the film, cries at its mother-daughter scene and sleeps beside him, with no new relationship milestone ([Ch093](../../chapters/093-movie-night.md#L539), [Ch093](../../chapters/093-movie-night.md#L641), [Ch093](../../chapters/093-movie-night.md#L775), [Ch093](../../chapters/093-movie-night.md#L883)). She helps protect the birch-grove boundary on the public map ([Ch097](../../chapters/097-the-map.md#L379)); at the thousandth campfire she supports Grover's speech, which uses her feedback, and celebrates with him ([Ch100](../../chapters/100-the-thousandth-campfire.md#L419), [Ch100](../../chapters/100-the-thousandth-campfire.md#L589), [Ch100](../../chapters/100-the-thousandth-campfire.md#L633)).
- **Chapters 135, 139 and 143 — ecological work:** Juniper asks Grover to help untangle roots by the creek; she and Grover later prepare a seawater grounding pool for the storm response. When the dock repair avoids disturbing the naiads' shelf, Grover says Juniper will be relieved; that is his expectation, not proof she was present or told ([Ch135](../../chapters/135-the-garden.md#L163), [Ch139](../../chapters/139-storm-season.md#L211), [Ch143](../../chapters/143-the-dock-repair.md#L225)).
- **Knowledge and unknowns:** Juniper knows the serviceberry plan and the particular relationship conversations she attended. The delivery and receipt of Grover's quest-era messages remain unverified except where a later scene reports receipt. No scene gives her Percy or Annabeth's private remembered-life information. Her later grove and partner status remain as last shown above.
