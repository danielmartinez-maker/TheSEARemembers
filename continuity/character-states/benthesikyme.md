# Benthesikyme: source-limited continuity notes

**Coverage:** Confirmed scenes in Chapters 001, 086, 125–126, 129, and 131 only. This file is not a full character timeline or appearance audit; no locator sweep has been run.

## Established details

- Benthesikyme is a daughter of Poseidon, Percy’s sister, and lives in Atlantis from Chapter 001. Exact age, mother/other parentage, powers, and personal history are unspecified. No maternal relationship with Amphitrite is established.
- In Chapter 001, Amphitrite says Benthesikyme and Rhode have been asking when Percy will join them. At Percy’s departure, Benthesikyme asks whether he will come back. Percy promises. She and Rhode are his sisters, but he cannot recall their shared altered childhood.
- In Chapter 086, Benthesikyme is at home in Atlantis when Percy returns with the Camp Three visitors. She welcomes him after the visitors reunite with the palace family.
- In Chapters 125–126, she is present at the Atlantis family dinner and palace scenes as a resident, not a Camp Half-Blood traveler.
- In Chapter 129, Benthesikyme is included in the Atlantis garden family photograph. Chapter 131’s Atlantis portrait also includes her beside Triton.

## Scope boundary

“Camp Three siblings” means Percy, Clarisse, and Silena. Benthesikyme is not placed in Cabin Three or Camp Half-Blood by the Atlantis visit or camp portrait. Do not supply an age, power set, additional parentage, or Amphitrite title without source support.
