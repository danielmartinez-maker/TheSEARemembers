# Hazel Levesque: cumulative memory

**Entry coverage:** Source-verified appearances in Chapters 083 and 093 only; intervening chapters are not audited here.

## Verified cumulative timeline (Chapters 001–193)

- **Chapter 066 — reported, not firsthand to the Greeks:** Jason's letter to Thalia identifies Hazel as one of his closest friends, gives her age and Pluto parentage, and describes a Roman war-game outing with her. Thalia and the Greek readers know only what Jason chose to report ([Ch066](../../chapters/066-jason-s-letter.md#L157), [Ch066](../../chapters/066-jason-s-letter.md#L183)).
- **Chapter 073 — direct Camp Half-Blood visit:** Hazel plays Persephone in the camp production, sings the written spring melody, and offers gentle observations to Thalia during rehearsal. She is physically present with the visiting Roman group, not merely a figure in Jason's letters ([Ch073](../../chapters/073-the-play.md#L413), [Ch073](../../chapters/073-the-play.md#L435), [Ch073](../../chapters/073-the-play.md#L647), [Ch073](../../chapters/073-the-play.md#L939)).
- **Chapter 083 — spring exchange:** Hazel welcomes Annabeth, Percy and the others as host and established letter-writing friend. She helps Annabeth inspect a library annex and drainage conditions, keeps the cause of foundation movement explicitly unverified, and discusses a shared library using approved copies rather than surrendering original records. The engineer accepts the survey request but does not yet approve construction. Hazel later describes the temples and city, joins games and fireworks, lends Percy a small Roman gift, and carries the Roman copy of the library file home ([Ch083](../../chapters/083-spring-break-visit.md#L221), [Ch083](../../chapters/083-spring-break-visit.md#L477), [Ch083](../../chapters/083-spring-break-visit.md#L497), [Ch083](../../chapters/083-spring-break-visit.md#L525), [Ch083](../../chapters/083-spring-break-visit.md#L813), [Ch083](../../chapters/083-spring-break-visit.md#L849)).
- **Chapter 093 — bounded visit:** Hazel attends the supervised movie night with Frank and Jason. Her star-patterned blanket stays with her until she lends it to Ella, who returns it at first bell; Hazel also returns Percy's blue blanket. She and Frank share popcorn and a quiet conversation, with no new relationship agreement stated ([Ch093](../../chapters/093-movie-night.md#L262), [Ch093](../../chapters/093-movie-night.md#L791), [Ch093](../../chapters/093-movie-night.md#L893), [Ch093](../../chapters/093-movie-night.md#L945)).
- **Relationship and unknowns:** Hazel and Annabeth were already corresponding before their in-person meeting; later scenes preserve that acquaintance. The proposed shared library remains a future possibility, not a built or approved project. Hazel has not been shown learning Percy or Annabeth's private autobiographical memories.

**Entry coverage:** Source-verified reported contact in Chapter 066 and firsthand milestones in Chapters 073, 083 and 093. Incidental name mentions are not separate contact records; later knowledge is unknown.
