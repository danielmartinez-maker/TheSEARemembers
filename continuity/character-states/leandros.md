# Leandros: source-limited continuity notes

**Coverage:** Chapters 194–195 only; not a full appearance or relationship audit. Author-approved character baseline; not a full appearance or relationship audit.

## Author-approved identity

- Leandros is sixteen at introduction, a son of Poseidon and a separate mortal mother, and a paternal half-brother to Percy, Clarisse, and Silena.
- He is not an Atlantis resident and is not related through his mother to Sally, Maria, Beryl, or Amphitrite.
- His Camp Three placement begins in Chapter 194. He chooses the available cot for that night; longer-term placement is not settled.

## Confirmed in Chapter 194

- A satyr named Callum escorts Leandros to camp. His mortal mother accompanies him to the bus station and waits until Callum confirms the trail is safe; she wanted him to be safe and he says she did not want him to leave.
- He says a monster found him twice at his school marina. He permits that fact to be shared but withholds the creature’s appearance. No monster identity or continuing threat is established.
- A glowing trident sign confirms Poseidon’s paternity in front of Percy, Clarisse, Silena, Chiron, Callum, and Leandros.
- He reads a change in lake currents by observing surface wind and water. Treat this as a practical, narrow skill; no broad water mastery is demonstrated.
- He dismisses the braced fence and the cabin’s blanket insulation as inferior, assumes that camp needs one leader, and says he does not take orders from girls. Clarisse and Silena set clear boundaries. He has not apologized.
- He says he can cook from experience on boats; Chapter 195 demonstrates a simple cooking skill with mistakes and correction.
- At the chapter’s warm meal, he first dismisses Silena’s honey-cinnamon apples as excessive effort, then says they are good and takes another piece. This is a small change in behavior, not a trust milestone.
- He meets Percy, Clarisse, and Silena for the first time. Clarisse and Silena do not owe him instant closeness.
- He asks Percy about Atlantis. Percy says the subject is private; he does not disclose his memory gap or erased-future memories. Leandros does not know about the timeline, the rewrite, or the exclusive memory rule.

### Chapter 195 · The Rules of Cabin Three

- **Source:** [Chapter 195](../../chapters/195-the-rules-of-cabin-three.md).
- Leandros meets Annabeth for the first time; he does not claim a title or authority in that exchange.
- He first declines a shield orientation, then chooses to learn one stance and returns the equipment.
- He demonstrates simple skillet flatcakes. His first is too thick and unevenly cooked; he lowers the heat after Silena’s correction, and the next is better. His boat cooking experience is real but limited.
- He asks Silena directly to help with dinner and listens to practical correction. He still bristles when told his first attempt is wrong.
- He specifically acknowledges that he used his sexist “orders from girls” remark to reject Clarisse’s correction. This is a partial apology, not a trust repair. Clarisse does not promise trust or forgiveness and gives him no authority.
- He uses the Cabin Three cot again, leaves his duffel zipped under it, and has no settled long-term placement.
- His mother’s name and private history remain unspecified. The marina monster remains unidentified. He has not met Ianthe, Rhode, Benthesikyme, Triton, or Tyson through Chapter 195.

## Current handoff

At the close of Chapter 195, Leandros is still learning Cabin Three’s routines. Clarisse and Silena have set boundaries; neither owes him instant closeness. His pride, sexism when challenged, and status assumptions remain present despite a specific acknowledgment. Do not treat the flatcakes, shield stance, or apology as proof of broad competence or complete change. Preserve the difference between his mortal household and the Camp Three siblings’ unequal childhoods.
