# Ianthe: author-approved planned profile

**Status:** Not introduced in the manuscript through Chapter 194. The identity and character baseline below are author-approved for the planned family arc; do not convert outline beats into witnessed events.

## Approved identity

- Ianthe is fifteen at her planned introduction in Chapter 198.
- She is a daughter of Zeus and a separate mortal mother, and a paternal half-sister to Thalia, Annabeth, and Jason.
- She is not Beryl Grace’s daughter and does not share Beryl’s maternal history.
- She is planned to join Cabin One, subject to the arrival scene and camp logistics actually shown.

## Approved character baseline, not yet witnessed

- Zeus’s intermittent attention has encouraged her to mistake being specially chosen for having authority.
- She is bossy, status-conscious, and prejudiced toward campers she sees as undisciplined. She assumes Annabeth needs managing and Thalia is a problem to control.
- She is observant about wind and pressure and can plan carefully, but her plans fail when she does not consult the people doing the work.
- Growth is practical and partial: she must learn to ask, listen, and repair the costs of her commands. She does not become perfect, and Zeus does not become reliably present or safe.
- Her mortal mother remains her own parent. Her mother’s name, history, current location, contact pattern, and the precise arrival reason remain unknown until a chapter establishes them.

## Continuity boundary

As of the end of Chapter 194, no character in the manuscript has met Ianthe. No arrival, Zeus visit, weather event, memory disclosure, or relationship change involving her has occurred yet. Keep her outside the witnessed timeline until a scene shows otherwise.
