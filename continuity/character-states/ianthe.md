# Ianthe: source-limited continuity notes

**Coverage:** Selected source-anchored events in Chapters 198–206; this is not a full appearance or relationship audit. Author-approved baseline remains distinct from witnessed events.

## Author-approved identity

- Ianthe is fifteen at introduction, a daughter of Zeus and a separate mortal mother, and a paternal half-sister to Thalia, Annabeth, and Jason.
- She is not Beryl Grace’s daughter and does not share Beryl’s maternal history.
- Cabin One is her paternal family cabin. Her first-night room is the Big House guest room; her bunk and long-term arrangement remain unsettled.

## Author-approved baseline

- Zeus’s intermittent attention has encouraged her to mistake being specially chosen for having authority.
- She is bossy, status-conscious, and prejudiced toward campers she considers undisciplined. She assumes Annabeth needs managing and Thalia is a problem to control.
- She notices wind and pressure and plans carefully, but a plan can fail when she does not consult the people doing the work.
- Growth is practical and partial: she must learn to ask, listen, and repair the costs of her commands. She does not become perfect, and Zeus does not become reliably present or safe.
- Her mortal mother remains her own parent. Her mother’s name, history, location, and regular contact pattern remain unknown.
