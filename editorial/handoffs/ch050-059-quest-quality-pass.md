## Locked facts across all ten

- **Ages and families:** Percy and Annabeth are 12 during the quest; Thalia 16, Clarisse 13, Silena 14. Percy, Clarisse and Silena are Poseidon's biological children in Cabin Three; Percy is the youngest. Annabeth and Thalia are Zeus and Beryl's daughters in Cabin One; Jason is their brother, living apart with the Romans. Percy and Annabeth are **established romantic partners**, not brother and sister.
- **Memory boundary:** Only Percy and Annabeth remember their previous, seventeen-year-old timeline. Percy does **not** autobiographically remember growing up in underwater Atlantis in the revised life; other characters' accounts, his recent training and dreams must not be equated with restored childhood memories. Altered-life Sally died when Percy was two; original-life Sally raised him through childhood.
- **Authorship of time alteration remains unknown.** Kronos may be discussed as a mythic or historical adversary, but no one has established that he authored the timeline rewrite.
- **Quest roles:** Three are named by the Oracle (Percy, Annabeth, Grover). Thalia travels as Chiron's fourth, expressly approved guardian; this is an interpretation of the prophecy, not proof it is safe to bring four.
- **Zeus's restrictions:** Eagle escort, sky-gifts and extra storm assistance are withdrawn and **not automatically reinstated** because his master bolt is recovered. Annabeth and Thalia retain their inherent power with training constraints.
- **Travel:** Martin, an authorized **adult licensed driver**, operates the van after the nymph-depot handoff; Thalia navigates but must not suddenly drive public roads. Following the Los Angeles/Underworld passage, a separate authorized **court transport** assumes the route and custody of the weapon. No child carries the recovered bolt unsecured to Olympus.

## Consecutive chapter handoffs

### 050 Half-Blood Hill → 051 Medusa's Place
First dawn outside camp after the Ch049 quest preparations. Actual gear test sets limits and assignments; a Fury attacks the group. Percy/Annabeth improvise with water and electricity, but nearly injure their own group through an uncontrolled conductive path. Annabeth's hand needs care. Bus changes, money and Chiron's message prevent a magical fast-forward over transport. Grover writes Juniper and camp messages when safe. They need reliable onward travel; the depot and its authorized adult become the next logistical handoff.

### 051 Medusa's Place → 052 The Arch
A nymph depot arranges Martin to drive. Grover learns about a buzzing shipment from a trucker with a verifiable route. Medusa is defeated using a **reflected image to guide Percy's bronze strike**, not by looking at her own reflection and spontaneously turning to stone. Petrified victims, including Uncle Ferdinand, are triaged and identified; recovery-team transport and a documented contact replace the implausible plan to drive three heavy statues west to Los Angeles. The shipping lead remains uncertain, not proof Hades is the thief. Maintain Grover's anxiety and the open recovery case.

### 052 The Arch → 053 River
A courier-record lead motivates the hazardous visit to St. Louis; Annabeth recalls the previous timeline's Chimera fall but not infallible prophecy. Echidna and the Chimera attack. Annabeth and Grover help evacuate real civilians, Thalia holds the gallery, and Percy disobeys a stated plan to return before jumping into the Mississippi. His survival is difficult and physically costly, not automatic safety for all falls. Thalia sustains a head injury needing assessment, and all four require rest. Martin remains their driver and contact. Do not insert a telkhine into the Arch encounter.

### 053 River → 054 Lotus
The Mississippi spirit discusses older Atlantis tales with Percy but does not deliver missing autobiographical memories or identify the time architect. The original-life Sally grief persists. Camp's dead-drop message was prepared before the Arch and **cannot already describe the Arch disaster**. The post-Arch written report distinguishes eyewitness facts from conjecture. Thalia's contact anxiety about Jason grows without turning him into someone present in the scene. A clinic, hotel rest, injury follow-up and a factual report precede resumed travel.

### 054 Lotus → 055 Underworld Gates
The Lotus casino exploits actual needs: Sally's lost voice and childhood, Thalia's desire for ordinary time with Jason, Grover's nature and Annabeth's attraction to solvable architecture. The group escapes, losing **five days**; the deadline is now the following solstice day. Time loss is established by external dates/tickets, not just feelings. Martin is also vulnerable as a mortal and must escape with the party. He remains licensed driver toward Los Angeles. Avoid reintroducing duplicated declarations of love as filler.

### 055 Underworld Gates → 056 Hades
Los Angeles and the Underworld entrance are reached under a concrete, finite timeline. Martin signs travel handoffs and stays in an authorized waiting/contact role rather than magically transporting children into Hades's court. Percy submits a separate **records/welfare inquiry** about altered-life Sally. Entering Hades's domain does not automatically grant a reunion with her. Charon's administrative and passage rules matter. Four living travelers descend together with a clear limit on what they can request.

### 056 Hades → 057 Ares
Hades reports both the theft of Zeus's bolt and his **own stolen Helm of Darkness**. The helm is revealed inside the questers' gear without proven chain of custody; Hades's property is returned, and he replies to Sally's inquiry without giving Percy full access or rewriting grief. Bianca and Nico exist under Hades's guardianship; their residence and movements are private. Hades and the questers must not be made to swear confidently to facts they have not observed. Ares becomes the practical next suspect, but a broader instigator and any connection to the rewound timeline remain unproved.

### 057 Ares → 058 Olympus
At Santa Monica on **June 20**, the confrontation with Ares leads to recovery of the master bolt. It is secured in a containment vessel by authorized court personnel and transported under separate guard toward Manhattan. The children's injuries and observations are recorded. **Percy does not keep the unsecured bolt on his knees or in his bag.** The formal council appearance is on the **June 21 summer solstice**.

### 058 Olympus → 059 Homecoming
Zeus accepts the bolt, avoiding immediate divine war, while unresolved political tensions and restrictions remain. Court documents and custody records are delivered; Poseidon and Amphitrite can acknowledge Percy without curing the difference between his two remembered lives. Sally's welfare reply does not resurrect or release her. Zeus remains volatile, frequently absent and inconsistent, and any brief tenderness cannot cancel that pattern.

### 059 Homecoming → 060 Summer's End
The group returns to Camp Half-Blood on the following day. Silena and Clarisse continued patrols, equipment, boats and camp work while Percy was absent; Percy, Annabeth, Thalia and Grover have injuries requiring realistic rest or follow-up. A first homecoming ceremony does not close the cases of petrified victims or the Lotus guests. Grover and Juniper plan a living serviceberry planting while Uncle Ferdinand remains under specialist care, **not declared cured or dead**. Nico and Bianca visit with **explicit supervision, consent and privacy rules**; they have not permanently joined the camp by default. Percy/Annabeth's relationship was already established before their return; the campfire is **not** a new first-time boyfriend/girlfriend negotiation.

Ch060 begins later, during the **last week of summer**, after an interval: preserve injury recovery and time elapsed before declaring everybody fit for sparring. Verify changes in ages and school year across that interval rather than carrying Ch059's twelve-year-old ages indefinitely.
