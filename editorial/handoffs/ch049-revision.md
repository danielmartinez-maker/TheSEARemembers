## Quest roster and canon
- **Three named questers:** Percy (sea-born), Annabeth (storm-born), Grover (wild hoof-worn). **Fourth traveling companion:** Thalia, allowed by Chiron as a **guardian, not a replacement or unambiguously named fourth quester**. This distinction is a conditional interpretation, **not a guarantee** that four travelers are safe under the prophecy. The original roster is preserved into ch050, where Chiron calls all four to the porch.
- **Prophecy:** “Three shall go west ... owl and trident share one line.” Annabeth's former self was an Athena daughter, but her present identity is **Zeus/Beryl**. Percy and Annabeth privately recognize that “owl” is ambiguous; they must not assert that the Oracle has certified their former life or disclosed the time-rewrite architect.
- **Continuity from 048:** Zeus withdrew **sky-gifts / eagle escort / storm aid**. Chiron and the group account for those constraints explicitly. **Poseidon has not replied** by the planning meeting. Tyson is **still** visiting from the undersea forges, not independently arriving again as originally misdescribed.
- **Family and responsibilities:** Clarisse and Silena commit to keeping the camp functioning, including border watches, equipment repair and handoffs. Tyson helps with boat maintenance only during his existing visit. Silena's food and first aid provisions are prepared and labeled; Grover sets achievable contact expectations with Juniper and discusses limits to satyr sensory warning on roads; Thalia and Annabeth negotiate guardian-versus-commander boundaries without fully resolving their disagreement.
- **Rachel:** Following delivery of the prophecy, she asserts autonomy and declines repeated individual fortune demands. Chiron helps her post a visitor boundary in her own words. She **does not supply a second prophecy or claim hidden interpretive knowledge**.
- **Narrative viewpoint:** Third-person limited Percy. Removed original final-night omniscient claim to know what Annabeth was doing in Cabin One and unearned narrative certainty about the unseen foe in the west.
- **Time:** Morning after Zeus's public visit, with **departure scheduled for the following dawn** in ch050. Ch050 begins before the conch. Do not collapse the overnight handoff.
