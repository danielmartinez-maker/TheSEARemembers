# Prose and dialogue acceptance standard

**Applies to:** Every revised narrative chapter in the 193-chapter 10k expansion project.

## Primary rule

**Length is not acceptance.** A chapter passes only when its scenes develop consequentially, its prose reads naturally, and each speaker sounds like a specific person rather than participating in an exchange of interchangeable one-liners.

## Dialogue

- Avoid chains of brief question/answer pairs, one-word replies, and repeated quips that feel like a screenplay outline. Use sustained answers, selective interruptions, meaningful deflection, and physical tasks to vary rhythm. Short lines may remain when stress, wit, or deliberate reticence *earns* them; a cluster of five is an editing warning rather than an automatic error.
- Distinguish registers. Percy can be quick, irreverent and unusually compassionate without delivering therapy speeches; Annabeth is analytically exacting but not perpetually formal; Thalia defends her sister with guarded bluntness and anger that may outlast apologies; Clarisse is practical and proud, rarely volunteering vulnerability; Silena notices what others need and still has her own work, impatience and preferences.
- Never have everybody respond in therapeutic jargon, announce how they feel, or immediately state the lesson a scene taught them.
- Dialogue should not be *all* banter or *all* exposition. Let conflict arise from conflicting objectives, interruptions and competing duties. Characters can misunderstand one another; they don't always resolve the dispute before the scene ends.
- A character's silence or short refusal is useful only if the surrounding scene makes its meaning clear. Avoid every speaker getting the same number of turns.

## Prose, flow and scene rhythm

- Preserve natural third-person limited voice. Use one focalizer per scene; switch only after a clearly marked separator.
- Alternate scene lengths and densities. Vary sentence cadence, paragraph length and the ratio of spoken language, action and interiority. Do not manufacture short sentences solely to sound dramatic.
- The POV character notices what they have reasons to notice. Avoid broad inventories of weather, scent, clothing or emotional sensations repeated in adjacent paragraphs.
- Do not explain a gesture immediately after showing it if the gesture and context already convey the meaning.
- Do not rely repeatedly on characters waking, meeting on a dock, exchanging assurances, hugging and ending with lyrical pronouncements. Preserve quiet camp intimacy but give each gathering a distinct purpose and persistent consequence.
- Every added scene must change information, trust, an obligation, skill, decision or relationship. Expansion through descriptions alone does not qualify.
- Maintain Percy/Annabeth's preexisting nonsexual relationship, sibling boundaries, altered-family canon, characters' distinct motivations and knowledge limitations. Consult adjacent chapters to avoid repetition and premature payoff.
- Avoid over-explaining the central time mystery. Neither old memories nor new observations establish the unknown architect's identity.
- Avoid excessive em dashes, fragmented paragraphs and repeated constructions such as "not X, but Y"; ordinary coordination and natural contractions are welcome.

## Review procedure

For each chapter, perform **two distinct edits** beyond drafting:
1. **Scene/developmental edit** for causality, distinct character wants, pacing, emotional credibility, adjacent chapter handoffs and the avoidance of duplicated scenes.
2. **Line/dialogue edit** reading dialogue exchanges in context, noting repetitive alternating short turns and transitions that read like screenplay cues. Read scenes in the voice of their actual viewpoint character.

Then recount body words (minimum 10,000), check the verbatim 300-word sliding-window overlaps, confirm canon/POV/age/objects and update the handoff. The word-count script is only a length gate, not a stylistic scoring model.

**Progress notation:** A chapter over 10,000 words that has not yet passed both editorial reads is `length pass, editorial review pending`, not `approved`.
