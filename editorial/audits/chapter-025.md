## Findings

### 025-1 · Moderate

**Evidence:** [L67](../../chapters/025-night-watches.md#L67). Night-numbered titled sections work as a week-long montage but function as internal scene labels, which the strict bible discourages.

**Suggested repair:** If retaining a week-calendar format, treat labels as an expressly authorized exception; otherwise use date cues inside prose and `---` breaks.

### 025-2 · High

**Evidence:** [L595–629](../../chapters/025-night-watches.md#L595-L629). Percy and Annabeth fall asleep together on the Big House porch, Argus sends them to their cabins, then Annabeth whispers goodnight in her bunk and Percy seems to answer from *another cabin*. There is no audible communication channel.

**Suggested repair:** Make the final greetings parallel thoughts rather than a conversation, or stage their exchange before separating.

### 025-3 · Moderate

**Evidence:** [L23–27](../../chapters/025-night-watches.md#L23-L27). The sign-up board specifies three standing pairs; the chapter covers Night One from both Percy and Annabeth positions. Fine in itself, but the relationship between daily schedule/rotating duties and which patrol pair is on at each time needs a stable list.

**Suggested repair:** Record the seven-night rota so later references do not accidentally put the same person on two simultaneous duties.

### 025-4 · Moderate

**Evidence:** [L303–591](../../chapters/025-night-watches.md#L303-L591). Repeated patrols conclude with conversation, snacks and warmth, frequently without the expected observations about the increasingly restless borders.

**Suggested repair:** Let at least one night produce distinct security intelligence or a changed patrol protocol, not only closer friendships.

### 025-5 · Low

**Evidence:** [L703–713](../../chapters/025-night-watches.md#L703-L713). The collective breakfast declares that six watchers have become closer, though most scenes already demonstrate this concretely.

**Suggested repair:** Shorten the final generalized takeaway in favor of the particular exchange between Silena, Clarisse and Annabeth.

## Material worth keeping

The pairings are well chosen to produce nonromantic closeness. Clarisse/Annabeth and Percy/Thalia compare family histories without treating kinship as interchangeable.
