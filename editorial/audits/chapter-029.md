### 029-01 · Zoe chronology

**Source:** [289–305](../../chapters/029-rainy-day.md#L289-L305). Zoe is said to have lived two thousand years and spends the rainy day among Artemis/Hunter-associated girls while described elsewhere as a daughter of Hecate and a former Hunter. **Source:** [3–94, 415–498](../../chapters/029-rainy-day.md#L3-L94). The episode covers three consecutive rainy days before returning to ordinary camp. Record the duration so the following visit and camp events do not compress or repeat those dates.

**Source:** [97–170](../../chapters/029-rainy-day.md#L97-L170). Silena's boredom in the Aphrodite cabin is legitimate friendship, not evidence she remains Aphrodite's daughter. Preserve her Poseidon parentage and give her initiative beyond mediating others' feelings.

**Source:** [173–232, 349–412](../../chapters/029-rainy-day.md#L173-L232). The chapter changes focal characters seven times for closely related indoor diversions. Preserve the distinctive rainy-day rituals, but decide whether every viewpoint adds a different decision rather than another description of collective coziness.
