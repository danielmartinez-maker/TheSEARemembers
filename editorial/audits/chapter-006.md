## Editorial verdict
**Strong potential as a Thalia-focused chapter, but not currently continuous with Chapter 5.** The opening provides credible observations of Annabeth's changed behavior and Thalia's protectiveness. Then events and beliefs appear to reset: Thalia seems not to know about Nico's public accusation, and Percy introduces Zeus as if for the first time. The prose's unusually abstract formal dialogue damages voice distinction and violates the bible's natural-dialogue rule.
