## Main confirmed results

### 1. Critical 308-word duplicate in Chapter 191

[Chapter 191 lines 391–413](../../chapters/191-two-hands-of-work.md#L391-L413) repeat at [lines 417–439](../../chapters/191-two-hands-of-work.md#L417-L439). The second Silena encounter repeats the same soup, path-step, door and cleaning conversation. A rolling-token scan identified **308 consecutive verbatim tokens** from the repeated passage. See the [Chapter 191 report](chapter-191.md) for the full repair plan. This is a **confirmed assembly defect**; edit the later occurrence coherently, not through an indiscriminate global deletion.

### 4. Long-range storyline and prose checks

- **The erased-life mystery:** Percy/Annabeth's retained memories and their changed families are governing canon. Exact references to Poseidon's “old gods” and “sacred places” warning appear only in [Chapter 1](../../chapters/001-the-weight-of-salt-and-sky.md#L118-L138) in this compiled import. This is an **open setup/payoff question**, not proof that the intended payoff was abandoned.
- **Ages:** Annabeth's fourteenth birthday is drafted in Chapter 133; Percy's in Chapter 136. Chapter 137 follows as a separate “Fourteen” chapter. From Chapter 140 onward both are fourteen unless another dated birthday changes it.
- **Thalia/Rhys:** Rhys is foreshadowed in [Ch 175 L465](../../chapters/175-salt-and-steam.md#L465), physically returns by [Ch 177 L37](../../chapters/177-the-one-who-came-back.md#L37), and appears in subsequent late chapters. Verify his own duties, flaws and goals in addition to the romance; Thalia and Rhys are adults in this late stretch.
- **Winter detail and verbal repetition:** Chapter 193 mentions the exact phrase “the winter was coming” on **13 lines**, and “the tenth lantern” on **14 lines**. A motif can recur intentionally, but each recurrence must justify its effect. Its 57 lines containing “heel” need a focus pass to separate developing injury evidence from restatement.
- **Progression:** The user designated no new arc boundary at Chapters 164, 172 or 193. Chapter 193's injury/winter setup remains ongoing. Do not evaluate it as a completed finale.

## Editorial priorities

1. **Fix actual assembly/canon breaches first:** the 308-word repeat in Ch 191, remaining parentage/memory/age faults, and physical scene-order defects already tracked in Chapters 1–66.
2. **Create and maintain one canonical continuity ledger:** ages/months, Jason/Tyson/Triton/Amphitrite visits, Sally vs Amphitrite memory sources, injuries, gift/object chains, cabin rules, power limits, ongoing projects.
3. **Decide what Chapters 101–114 are:** intentionally short linked interludes or underdeveloped chapters. Preserve the stronger small scenes rather than expand mechanically.
4. **Apply the per-chapter word floor coherently** to 140–164 and 191/193. Recheck any chapter after removing duplicates.
5. **Edit formulaic late prose** without deleting domestic detail that reveals character. Retain differentiated voices, earned conflict and practical work.
6. **Track the open time-rewrite mystery** separately from the resolved Lightning Thief bolt plot, and strengthen the later Thalia/Rhys arc with Rhys's independent personality.
