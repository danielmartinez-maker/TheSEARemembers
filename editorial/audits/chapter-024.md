### 024-2 · High

**Suggested repair:** Record her Hunter departure and Hecate-daughter status as an AU change, then give a natural in-story reference rather than an unexplained résumé.

**Suggested repair:** Show a distinctive shared gesture or tactical cue and allow occasional mistaken assumptions despite deep familiarity.
