## Editorial verdict
**Major canon and mystery-mechanics repair required before line editing.** The Hall provides a concrete institutional symbol, and the alternate prophecy variants have potential, but the investigation advances through implausibly accessible answers. Nico's claim to remember the previous world is a direct conflict with the story bible's locked rule unless the author explicitly changes that rule.
