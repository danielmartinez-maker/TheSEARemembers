## Assessment
**Editorial disposition:** The drakon opening offers physical danger and meaningful Thalia/Annabeth tension, but later segments contradict who was present, relax the memory rules, introduce an implausibly transparent Chiron, and reset the emotional conversation repeatedly.

Annabeth's storm powers are new skills, not inherited conscious training. **Relationship:** Percabeth is established and nonsexual. Their disagreement over privacy needs consequence rather than simply reassurance. **Length:** ~5,326 body words, early legacy chapter; the 10,000-word *new drafting* minimum is not presumed retroactive.
