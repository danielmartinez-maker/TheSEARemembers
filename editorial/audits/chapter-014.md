## Findings

### 014-1 · Critical

**Evidence:** [L41–65](../../chapters/014-letters-from-under-the-sea.md#L41-L65). Jason is physically present by the lake helping initiate an Iris-message, despite Chapter 13 having established that his supervised Camp Jupiter visit ended. There is no new permission or second arrival.

**Suggested repair:** Either place this scene during the established three-day visit through explicit timeline ordering or describe a new authorized trip and departure.

### 014-2 · High

**Evidence:** [L31–33](../../chapters/014-letters-from-under-the-sea.md#L31-L33). Percy says his first three outbound letters have received no reply, yet later says he received a letter from Poseidon last week. These can coexist if the timing/source differs, but the narrative does not explain it.

**Suggested repair:** Anchor which letters are outbound, which Poseidon note arrived, and why the narrator calls the postal exchange unanswered.

### 014-3 · High

**Evidence:** [L157–205](../../chapters/014-letters-from-under-the-sea.md#L157-L205). Poseidon appears via Iris-message and speaks to all three of his children, while Ch 7 introduces an unexplained cryptic letter implying he is lost and cannot be located.

**Suggested repair:** Select a coherent Poseidon availability/mystery status; do not silently preserve both incompatible suggestions.

### 014-4 · Moderate

**Evidence:** [L87–151](../../chapters/014-letters-from-under-the-sea.md#L87-L151). Clarisse and Silena suddenly receive unusually attentive words from Poseidon's household. Their understandable joy is clear, but the structural inequity of his earlier absence needs continued consequences.

**Suggested repair:** Let both sisters respond differently, avoid one family call curing years of neglect.

### 014-5 · Moderate

**Evidence:** [L355–470](../../chapters/014-letters-from-under-the-sea.md#L355-L470). The chapter spends a long stretch unpacking family sentiment through repeated close observations and journal reflection.

**Suggested repair:** Keep the call, the evening rituals and one decisive sibling choice; pare back reiterations of the same emotional insight.

## Strengths worth retaining

The Iris-message allows Amphitrite to treat Clarisse and Silena as distinct people rather than instantly claiming motherhood, and Triton's banter differentiates Atlantis from camp (L73–151).

## Handoff to Chapter 15

Iris-contact reaches Amphitrite, Triton and Poseidon, with sister interactions and unhealed paternal inequality. Clarify Jason's physical location and reconcile Poseidon's letters before revising downstream scenes.

**Working rule:** No new memories for anyone except Percy/Annabeth, keep Poseidon and Zeus families distinct, do not identify the timeline architect by inference. No source prose edited.
