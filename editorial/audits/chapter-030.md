## Findings and proposed repairs

### 030-01 · Memory-rule fault

**Source:** [79–83](../../chapters/030-amphitrite-visits.md#L79-L83). Percy narrates that he has previously seen Atlantean arrival processions. The altered-childhood memory rule requires a source such as recent visits or other relatives' descriptions; he cannot automatically remember those processions.

### 030-02 · Geography and mechanics

**Source:** [83–101](../../chapters/030-amphitrite-visits.md#L83-L101). Amphitrite deliberately arrives through the freshwater canoe lake, with a royal ocean chariot. This can be magic, but establish the route/portal mechanism because the chosen route is an unusual logistical exception.

### 030-03 · Parental history

**Source:** [267–336](../../chapters/030-amphitrite-visits.md#L267-L336). Clarisse receives Amphitrite's attention against a history of Poseidon's uneven care. Avoid presenting a single pleasant encounter as resolving her long-standing exclusion.

### 030-04 · Visitor ledger

**Source:** [523–575](../../chapters/030-amphitrite-visits.md#L523-L575). Tyson is physically at the family dinner, along with other guests. Record whether this is a supervised short visit, how he arrived and when he leaves; Chapter 23's earlier unlogged arrival remains open.

### 030-05 · Tessera memory attribution

**Source:** [677–707](../../chapters/030-amphitrite-visits.md#L677-L707). The earlier wording gave Percy a specific memory of losing to Amphitrite during the week before camp and implied repeated recent play in Atlantis, although no source established those events. The candidate now keeps Amphitrite's childhood account with her and shows Percy and his sisters working through the game in the present.

## Material to preserve

Amphitrite's public arrival and separate private conversations with Clarisse and Silena are essential to the blended-family premise.

## October 17 repairs

- The Atlantean pageant is described as Percy's imagined expectation, not a procession he remembers witnessing in altered childhood.
- The Tessera passage no longer grants Percy a pre-camp memory of losing to Amphitrite or repeated unanchored play in Atlantis; the visible game and his reaction occur in the present scene.
- The Chiron conversation concerns ordinary adjustment to camp; it does not make Amphitrite aware of Percy's altered-childhood memory gap or erased-future memories.

## Continuity handoff

Carry the established events and relationships above forward; log unresolved visitor, age, evidence, and memory questions rather than inventing a timeline retcon. Percy/Annabeth retain old-life memories, others do not; the time architect remains unknown. These focused repairs do not certify all prose or continuity in Chapter 030.
