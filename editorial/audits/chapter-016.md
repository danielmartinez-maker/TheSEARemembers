## Findings

### 016-1 · Moderate

**Evidence:** [L97–130](../../chapters/016-capture-the-flag.md#L97-L130). Zoe is presented as a Hecate child and capable border patrol specialist with archery/hunting mannerisms. These are compatible with the AU, but her distinctive prior Hunter status (eventually claimed in Ch 24) is not established with dates or departure.

**Suggested repair:** Maintain a backstory reference: when did Zoe join camp, and when/why did she leave the Hunters? Do not assume canon biography automatically carries over.

### 016-2 · Moderate

**Evidence:** [L155–189](../../chapters/016-capture-the-flag.md#L155-L189). Grover/Ella provide live commentary throughout an ordinary capture-the-flag game, sometimes turning the scene into a narrated sporting event that displaces immediate Percy/Annabeth stakes.

**Suggested repair:** Use commentary selectively for comic relief and let tactical beats stand without a running broadcast.

### 016-3 · Moderate

**Evidence:** [L315–351](../../chapters/016-capture-the-flag.md#L315-L351). Percy and Annabeth anticipate each other's movements because of the old-life partnership; that is legitimate. Some participants' conclusions about their intimacy become instant public certainty from brief looks.

**Suggested repair:** Let other campers interpret incomplete information imperfectly; don't make everyone a mind reader.

### 016-4 · Moderate

**Evidence:** [L653–727](../../chapters/016-capture-the-flag.md#L653-L727). End-of-game celebration offers friendship, food and tender Percabeth, but the chapter recaps closeness verbally after play already shows it.

**Suggested repair:** Prefer the concrete post-game exchange and trim generalized assurances.

### 016-5 · Low

**Evidence:** [L1–25](../../chapters/016-capture-the-flag.md#L1-L25). Team names and cabin loyalties are invented by the AU, which is allowed, but the final 'Poseidon-ish, Zeus-ish, Pizza' report obscures which side actually won.

**Suggested repair:** Leave the comedic name if desired but identify the winning team and scoring rules clearly.
