## Findings

### 020-1 · Moderate

**Source:** [L15–25](../../chapters/020-cake-and-lightning.md#L15-L25). The text calls this a 'belated sixteenth party' while later scenes frame it as Thalia's birthday day. She was already sixteen at the opening of the story, so the actual birthday and belated celebration need a date.

**Recommended fix:** Distinguish a delayed party for an already-passed birthday from the birthday itself; add a timeline entry.

### 020-2 · Moderate

**Source:** [L49–73](../../chapters/020-cake-and-lightning.md#L49-L73). The narrator says Silena's leadership makes Percy understand why **Aphrodite** children are followed into battle, and Clarisse's section relies on Ares-related archetypes. The AU explicitly reassigns both to Poseidon.

**Recommended fix:** Keep recognizable old-life comparisons only when Percy consciously recalls them; otherwise describe present-day Poseidon children without implying their old divine parentage is still current.

### 020-3 · Moderate

**Source:** [L67–133](../../chapters/020-cake-and-lightning.md#L67-L133). The party setup spends multiple scenes on logistical slapstick before producing an emotional change, repeating 'Clarisse doesn't do cute' and failed-baking beats.

**Recommended fix:** Keep the best practical mishap and give each preparation scene a new decision or relationship consequence.

### 020-4 · Moderate

**Source:** [L313–411](../../chapters/020-cake-and-lightning.md#L313-L411). Annabeth's hand-made gift and Thalia's reaction are the strongest beats; repeated authorial explanation of how the whole camp 'showed up' restates what the assembled gifts and imperfect party already demonstrate.

**Recommended fix:** Let the gifts, behavior and one private sister conversation carry the effect.

### 020-5 · Low

**Source:** [L747–793](../../chapters/020-cake-and-lightning.md#L747-L793). The ending adds an additional post-party reflection after the bonfire scene reaches a natural emotional close.

**Recommended fix:** Select the more distinctive of the two closure beats.
