## Findings

### 019-1 · Critical

**Source:** [L41–85](../../chapters/019-the-brother-across-the-country.md#L41-L85). Jason's Iris-message is staged as their first permitted contact (“They never let us see him before”), whereas Chapter 12 explicitly shows Jason physically visiting for three days and Chapter 14 shows him physically at the lake again.

**Recommended fix:** Rewrite Ch 19 as meaningful **renewed** contact, or move earlier and repair the other two chapters' visit timeline.

### 019-2 · High

**Source:** [L3](../../chapters/019-the-brother-across-the-country.md#L3). The chapter inserts `### Thalia`, `### Annabeth`, `### Percy`, and `### Annabeth / Percy` headers inside story prose, directly contrary to the story bible's no POV-label requirement.

**Recommended fix:** Remove POV captions, retain `---` breaks and make a single identifiable viewpoint per scene.

### 019-3 · High

**Source:** [L371–429](../../chapters/019-the-brother-across-the-country.md#L371-L429). The combined ‘Annabeth / Percy’ POV header blurs strict third-person limited at the closing drawing-table sequence.

**Recommended fix:** Select one focal character and reveal the other's responses through dialogue/actions.

### 019-4 · Moderate

**Source:** [L89–163](../../chapters/019-the-brother-across-the-country.md#L89-L163). Annabeth's missing memories of Jason's altered-family childhood correctly differ from the picture Thalia carries. The nickname 'Beff' is introduced and rapidly becomes a shared shorthand.

**Recommended fix:** Ground who taught Jason the nickname and how often they have actually contacted him; preserve the distinction between Thalia's memory and Annabeth's reconstructed knowledge.

### 019-5 · Moderate

**Source:** [L315–367](../../chapters/019-the-brother-across-the-country.md#L315-L367). Thalia receives a large emotional payoff in the contact and Percy comforts her afterwards. The writing resolves the longing rapidly by a consoling speech.

**Recommended fix:** Let a concrete plan for future calls or visits carry the hopeful change, without making separation harmless.

## Strengths to protect

Jason's distinct Camp Jupiter environment, fear of separation and the sisters' differing memories are emotionally strong material. The call itself could work very well once aligned with the earlier visit.

## Continuity handoff

Jason is physically based at Camp Jupiter; visit logistics are an authorial continuity problem, not evidence of a new timeline rewrite. The siblings have at least one Iris-message and a drawing shared afterward.

**Disposition:** Resolve causality and canon before polishing. Keep explicit author choices and documented unresolved questions separate. No prose edits in this pass.
