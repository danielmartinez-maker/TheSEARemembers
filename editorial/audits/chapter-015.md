## Findings

### 015-1 · Moderate

**Evidence:** [L63–97](../../chapters/015-beryl-s-letters.md#L63-L97). Beryl's letter asserts 38 days sober and gives an AA sponsor. The claim belongs to Beryl, not an objectively verified recovery status.

**Suggested repair:** Keep Thalia's rational mistrust and track verification over time, not immediate parental redemption.

### 015-2 · High

**Evidence:** [L175–190](../../chapters/015-beryl-s-letters.md#L175-L190). Thalia recalls Beryl promising a reunion two years earlier, while Annabeth examines photographs and written accounts she cannot remember living. Distinguish Thalia's memory from Annabeth's reconstructed knowledge in every POV.

**Suggested repair:** Maintain the missing-childhood gap and don't let poignant photographs produce full memories.

### 015-3 · Moderate

**Evidence:** [L5–31](../../chapters/015-beryl-s-letters.md#L5-L31). The chapter places Beryl's letter amid an emotionally crowded breakfast and then moves swiftly through decisions to process it. The ordinary domestic responses work but other campers sometimes become a chorus of competent advisers.

**Suggested repair:** Choose one central practical choice for Annabeth and Thalia and keep their reactions incompatible until earned.

### 015-4 · Moderate

**Evidence:** [L505–545](../../chapters/015-beryl-s-letters.md#L505-L545). The girls discuss a possible answer and Jason's whereabouts. A proposed letter has practical repercussions (safety, private address, whether Beryl learns camp/Jupiter logistics) that the scene only lightly explores.

**Suggested repair:** Treat any decision to reply as an accountable agreement; avoid revealing Jason's protected location casually.

### 015-5 · Low

**Evidence:** [L5](../../chapters/015-beryl-s-letters.md#L5). The story moves from a late-winter Zeus visit to a Friday 'a few weeks later' with strawberries and cold Sound air. Exact month and how this aligns with prior eclipse timing remain unclear.

**Suggested repair:** Add dates/seasons to the master ledger rather than retconning weather details line by line.

## Strengths worth retaining

This chapter distinguishes a mother who sends an optimistic recovery letter from the daughter who remembers a broken promise (L71–97, 182–190). The writing correctly acknowledges Annabeth cannot remember Thalia's childhood events firsthand.

## Handoff to Chapter 16

Beryl has written to both daughters and claims 38 days sober; Thalia knows a past promise was broken; Annabeth can know the story through Thalia or photos, not her own altered-life memory. Any contact with Beryl and information about Jason must be tracked.

**Working rule:** No new memories for anyone except Percy/Annabeth, keep Poseidon and Zeus families distinct, do not identify the timeline architect by inference. No source prose edited.
