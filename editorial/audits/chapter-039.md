## Findings and proposed repairs

### 039-01 · Critical locked-canon contradiction

**Evidence:** [L221–225](../../chapters/039-annabeth-s-project.md#L221-L225). Thalia explicitly says 'The Hunt had taught her to keep things short.' The altered-history Thalia never joined the Hunters; remove the claimed personal experience or attribute the approach to another teacher.

### 039-02 · Formatting/POV

**Evidence:** [L7–9, 245–247, 463–465](../../chapters/039-annabeth-s-project.md#L7-L9). There are numerous internal `### Character — Segment` labels, plus POV segments by Clarisse, Silena, Zoe and others. Use one perspective per scene and unlabeled breaks; keep only essential extra focalizers.

### 039-03 · Project timeline

**Evidence:** [L3–4, 365–450](../../chapters/039-annabeth-s-project.md#L3-L4). The shrine is announced as finished and dedicated following Chapter 33's approval. Confirm dryad permission, material safety, plans and construction work survive the jump in time.

### 039-04 · Zoe biography

**Evidence:** [L433–453](../../chapters/039-annabeth-s-project.md#L433-L453). Zoe invokes thousands of years, Artemis's Hunt and Hecate parentage in a first-person speech. These facts can coexist in an AU but need an explicit record of how and when she left the Hunt.
