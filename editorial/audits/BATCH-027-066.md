## Confirmed corrections: highest priority

1. **Thalia's age regression, Chapter 28.** [Line 147](../../chapters/028-sparring.md#L147) calls her **fifteen**. The approved baseline is **sixteen** at this stage. Her sixteenth birthday celebration appears elsewhere and requires a dated reconciliation.
2. **Thalia's impossible Hunter history, Chapter 39.** [Line 223](../../chapters/039-annabeth-s-project.md#L223) attributes a behavior to what “The Hunt had taught her.” In the changed timeline she **never joined the Hunters of Artemis**. Correct the line without changing the governing history.
3. **Verbatim duplicated romance dialogue, Chapter 54.** [Lines 541–547](../../chapters/054-lotus.md#L541-L547) duplicate Annabeth's promise word-for-word and restart Percy's answer. Remove the duplicate as a coherent scene repair, preserving the affection and conversation.
4. **Annabeth/Percy age regression, Chapter 62.** Chapter 61 explicitly calls both characters thirteen ([L359](../../chapters/061-fall.md#L359) and [L493](../../chapters/061-fall.md#L493)), while [Chapter 62, line 97](../../chapters/062-snow-day.md#L97) calls both **twelve**. Restore the thirteen-year working baseline until another dated birthday.
5. **Sally/Atlantis autobiographical memory distinction.** Chapter 30 ([L79](../../chapters/030-amphitrite-visits.md#L79)) narrates Percy having seen ceremonial Atlantean arrivals. Chapter 52 ([L125](../../chapters/052-the-arch.md#L125)) describes Triton's training in a way that risks reintroducing inaccessible altered-childhood memories. Tie such knowledge to recent training, direct observation, or family testimony.

## Serious continuity questions requiring a ledger, not a speculative retcon

- **Visitors and locations.** Jason's Camp Jupiter visits are now explicitly described in Chapter 43 ([L85](../../chapters/043-the-unsigned-note.md#L85)), and Tyson's short visits and Atlantis residence recur across Chapters 30–41 and 60–66. Establish a dated arrival/departure log. The explicit visits are **not** by themselves contradictions; missing transitions between them may be.
- **Transport and quest inventory.** Chapter 50 ends at a bus depot, while Chapter 51 opens with Thalia driving a van. Chapter 51 also mentions partially petrified civilian passengers in the van near its ending; later travel needs a disposition for them. Chapter 55 establishes **four** escape pearls, one for each traveler. Track the van, rescued travelers, pearl inventory, injuries, and transported artifacts through the Underworld, Ares confrontation, and Olympus.
- **Season and school year.** Pre-quest March and June material leads into the June 20 Ares encounter and the June 21 solstice. Chapter 61 begins a new school year in September. Chapter 62 is the **following winter**, while Chapters 63–66 take place in the following March–April. The calendar must separate these repeated seasons instead of grouping all spring/winter scenes as one year.
- **Annabeth's building and shrines.** Chapter 33's community project and Chapter 39's dedication are explicitly for camp and its people; Chapter 63 describes altars to Athena and Zeus in a hillside grove. Determine if these are different structures before altering the description.
- **Zeus's punishments and apologies.** Chapter 48 specifically withdraws his daughters' sky aid; Chapter 58 includes an awkward public thank-you. The latter does not cancel a history of unreliable and volatile parenting. Track whether gifts, sanctions and access were actually restored.
- **Story hierarchy.** The theft/Kronos sequence does **not** identify the architect who rewrote Percy and Annabeth's childhoods. No character other than Percy/Annabeth may acquire erased-history memories without an explicit author decision.

## Intentional choices not to "correct"

- **Four travelers for a three-person prophecy:** Chapter 49 expressly chooses Percy, Annabeth and Grover as the prophesied three, with Thalia accompanying as a defensive **guardian**. The distinction matters, but the headcount is **not** an accidental error.
- **Four Underworld pearls:** Chapter 55 deliberately establishes one for every traveler. This follows the approved fourth guardian exception.
- **Thematic domestic pacing:** The library archival day (27), sibling sparring (28), Amphitrite visit (30), Annabeth's project (33/39), Mythomagic competition (34), note exchange (43), stargazing (46), spring dance (64), and Triton's lesson (65) all support the author's romance-and-family-first preference. Do not automatically remove them because they are low-action scenes.
- **Jason's extensive existing correspondence:** Chapter 66 says Jason has written to Thalia for more than a year. Treat earlier letters and physical visits as existing contact; the new long letter is not the first reunion.

## Editorial sequencing recommendation

**Stage A: establish a canon-ledger baseline** for dates/ages, family/sibling status, visitors, letters, important gifts, bodily injuries, quest items, shrine construction and open mysteries. Backfill Chapters 1–26 instead of beginning a second incompatible timeline.

**Stage B: repair blocking contradictions** (age errors, Thalia/Hunt history, duplicate lines, memories without provenance, transport/cargo and school-year continuity).

**Stage C: revise craft** chapter by chapter with the author's preferred genre: natural voices, purposeful dialogue, distinct emotional shifts, earned privacy and affection, one clear focal viewpoint per scene. Many chapters benefit more from careful cuts than from additional speeches.

**Stage D: verify** all revised chapter files, cross-chapter handoffs and the assembled Markdown source. Make focused pull requests so no fixes silently overwrite the canon edition.
