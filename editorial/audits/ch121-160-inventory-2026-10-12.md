## Implemented manuscript changes

**121 (4,892 to 10,632):** Three substantial new scenes prior to the original sibling pact: Clarisse's independent arena drill and Percy's limits; Silena refusing unscheduled sewing work while designing her own case; Annabeth/Thalia's dispute about the washed-out shore steps, resolved by measurement and an actual Chiron-mandated closure. These scenes test whether family members can ask, refuse and still help one another.

**122–123 (remain short):** Atlantis departure is a proposed choice rather than a unilateral confirmed booking. Childhood palace memories Percy cannot possess are recast as Triton and Amphitrite's testimony and current imagination. The underwater garden is approached from saltwater Long Island Sound, never the freshwater canoe lake.

**140 (10,163):** At the midnight dock, siblings inspect the swim ladder, throw line, entry area and boundaries, and Annabeth has a meaningful choice to decline or participate, with Clarisse entering ahead of the others.

**141 (10,030):** After the contested canoe race, the teams discover defective and loose life vests, quarantine a bad jacket, account for cheating by Percy and Thalia, and propose a non-racing referee and marked course for a future race.

**142 (10,087):** After the night swim, Clarisse admits unease and the group plans to report a buoy/navigation concern to Chiron rather than treating its success as proof there was no risk.

**143 (10,136):** A new inspection beat identifies an inadequately supported washer before loading the repaired dock. Their own construction receives scrutiny rather than automatic approval.

**146 (10,089):** The patrol turns west-shore discoveries into a dated safety handoff to Chiron, with submerged stairs closed, a damaged retaining wall referred to the repair crew, and uncertainty marked on the map.

**147 (10,143):** Replaced several impossible erased-life memories attributed to Clarisse, Silena and Thalia with present-life conflicts. Reworked the Atlas drill so Percy stops at pain and learns a controllable recovery rather than holding for contradictory ten- and twenty-minute stretches. Percy and Annabeth retain their actual prior memories and grief. Source remains in need of a full developmental edit for repeated motifs.

**148 (10,293):** Olive's report that public map markings exposed the heron's nest prompts Annabeth to create distinct public and restricted surveys. This adds a real cartographic ethics issue and a record of what the public wall map withholds.

**149 (10,387):** Corrected an Ares camper falsely labeled Clarisse's sibling, removed erased-life claims attributed to both Clarisse and Thalia and the false reference to Thalia becoming a Hunter, and replaced a repetitive final reassurance scene with repair of the actual watch/training roster, restoring choices to younger campers.

**151 (10,823):** Removed references to Clarisse remembering Ares parentage in a previous life and Thalia remembering a tree/Hunter life. Their dialogue and Percy's follow-up now concern altered-present memories and family actions they could plausibly know.

**157 (10,134):** Silena checks the visitor bunk for Tyson and refrains from treating a temporary visit as an implied permanent arrangement.

**158 (10,149):** Percy/Tyson identify and record a dock fastening to be safely inspected in daylight.

**159 (10,133):** Silena accepts Clarisse's help with the heavy wet quilt and other work, instead of taking all care obligations on herself.

Unlisted chapters were not substantively revised in this pass. Note that work took place through multiple targeted commits; all writes preserved the requested existing branch, and 409 GitHub branch-head conflicts were handled by fresh reads and non-forced retries.
