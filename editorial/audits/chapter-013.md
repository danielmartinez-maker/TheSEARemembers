# Chapter 013: The Father Who Changes Faces

**Status:** Audit only, prose unchanged.  
**Source:** [The Father Who Changes Faces](../../chapters/013-the-father-who-changes-faces.md)  
**Approximate body words:** 6,228 (existing legacy chapter; new-chapter word floor is not retroactive).  
**Scope:** Continuity, canon, prose, relationship dynamics, and adjacent-chapter handoff.

## Findings

### 013-1 · High

**Evidence:** [L5](../../chapters/013-the-father-who-changes-faces.md#L5). The chapter states 'Tuesday morning in late winter' after Chapter 8 described summer humidity only days before the eclipse and Chapter 12 takes place five days after the eclipse. No clear months-long time lapse is established.

**Suggested repair:** Create a dated season ledger; either move the winter setting or explicitly establish an elapsed period.

### 013-2 · High

**Evidence:** [L83–97](../../chapters/013-the-father-who-changes-faces.md#L83-L97). Annabeth's behavioral list is framed as a practiced method for a father whose appearances she does not remember. This can reflect observed new behavior, but specific childhood coping strategies cannot be autobiographical in her present consciousness.

**Suggested repair:** Ground the list in recent visits, written notes or Thalia's testimony; separate learned current strategies from false childhood recall.

### 013-3 · Moderate

**Evidence:** [L167–173](../../chapters/013-the-father-who-changes-faces.md#L167-L173). Percy briefly knows an incident from his altered age-five childhood because Amphitrite told him. The text appropriately attributes it, but later passages should not convert it into Percy's own lived memory.

**Suggested repair:** Maintain the 'she told me' provenance in future references.

### 013-4 · Moderate

**Evidence:** [L317–339](../../chapters/013-the-father-who-changes-faces.md#L317-L339). Zeus admits to listening poorly, offers gifts and appears momentarily humble despite a long pattern of volatility. The event is plausible if followed by uneven or failed improvement.

**Suggested repair:** Keep the apology limited and contested; no redeemed-father shortcut after one lunch.

### 013-5 · Moderate

**Evidence:** [L379–469](../../chapters/013-the-father-who-changes-faces.md#L379-L469). The post-Zeus debrief with Percy and the sisters is strong but grants substantial emotional resolution and multiple assurances in the same evening.

**Suggested repair:** Retain the difficult private conversation and small actions; allow injury and anger to persist.

## Strengths worth retaining

Zeus's shifting appearance, Annabeth's numbered rules, and Thalia's refusal to let him redefine care clearly fit the locked family model (L79–97, 171–181, 239–319).

## Handoff to Chapter 14

Zeus visited in a new face; the girls challenged him, he gave small boxes and acknowledged failure without proving lasting change. Jason's *recent physical visit has ended* (L267). Future Jason visits require explicit scheduling.

**Working rule:** No new memories for anyone except Percy/Annabeth, keep Poseidon and Zeus families distinct, do not identify the timeline architect by inference. No source prose edited.
