## Findings and proposed repairs

### 036-01 · Timeline framing

**Evidence:** [L3–15](../../chapters/036-five-months.md#L3-L15). The opening says five months before the remembered bolt quest was their *initial estimate*, not the present date. Future recaps must preserve this distinction; the passage does not justify resetting five months of camp life.

### 036-02 · Age and school

**Evidence:** [L43–94](../../chapters/036-five-months.md#L43-L94). Annabeth's knowledge of the date and structured studying belong to the altered present. Track the calendar between winter scenes, Beryl's letters and the upcoming theft without treating remembered canon timing as fixed.

### 036-03 · Scene function

**Evidence:** [L97–204](../../chapters/036-five-months.md#L97-L204). Training and lake scenes foreground already established competencies. They need a small change in responsibility or expectation to distinguish this ordinary day from many earlier lake mornings.

### 036-04 · POV discipline

**Evidence:** [L207–398](../../chapters/036-five-months.md#L207-L398). The chapter successively uses Thalia, Clarisse, Grover and Percy as introspective lenses. With the bible's preferred Percy/Annabeth/Thalia focus, retain extra POV only when a scene shows something those three cannot convey.
