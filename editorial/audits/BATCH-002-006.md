## Cross-chapter repair dependencies

1. **Protect the sole two remembered-life perspectives.** Percy and Annabeth are the only holders of original-future memory. Ch 4 gives Nico pre-rewrite memories; Ch 5 treats Percy/Annabeth as though she does not remember him. Resolve before later arc revisions, since both errors generate false emotional stakes.
2. **Keep the sisters in the right families.** Thalia and Annabeth are Zeus daughters; Clarisse and Silena are Poseidon daughters with Percy. Ch 2 gives Thalia a winged-panther carving tradition; Ch 5 reassigns near-identical material to Clarisse and implicitly casts Annabeth as Clarisse's central sister. Differentiate their motivations and crafts. Annabeth may not be called a present-day “Athena girl.”
3. **Preserve evidence locations.** The edited prophecy transcript and a signed “A” note are recovered from the **Big House attic** (Ch 4), and copies are carried away. The **Hall of the Gods** is attacked in Ch 5. Hall destruction cannot automatically erase attic documents or carried records.
4. **Keep Zeus's guilt uncertain.** At the end of Ch 5, Nico accuses Zeus without displaying proof. Ch 6 restages the suspicion as Percy's new revelation to Thalia, even though Thalia heard Nico's accusation. Rebuild Ch 6 as the *reaction to a known accusation*, not a second first-time reveal. Neither Nico's certainty nor Percy's inference changes the governing rule that the architect remains unidentified.
5. **Track days and ages.** Early chapters use day-to-day scenes while also asserting two-week and three-week elapsed periods. Explicitly date Annabeth's initial memory event, Percy's arrival, the drakon attack, Nico's discovery and Thalia's first accusation. Clarisse (13) and Silena (14) cannot both have arrived at age five seven years earlier.
6. **Keep the chapter intentions.** The story is *fluff and romance first*, not an investigation serial with occasional hugs. Preserve the concrete emotional scenes; increase consequence, reciprocity and character-specific routines. Do not add padding merely to satisfy an early chapter's word total.

## Recommended revision order

**First:** Chapter 1's already-opened issue [#1](https://github.com/danielmartinez-maker/TheSEARemembers/issues/1), since it fixes the arrival sequence.

**Then:** Chapter 2 → Chapter 3 → Chapter 4 → Chapter 5 → Chapter 6, tracking decisions forward. **One focused pull request per chapter** allows corrections to be reviewed independently, with a final end-to-end continuity pass over Chapters 1–6.

### Length policy

The 10,000-body-word **minimum applies to new drafting assignments**. Per `FIC_CONTEXT.md`, earlier chapters remain as written unless expansion is explicitly authorized. These five legacy chapters are short; that is a descriptive fact, not an automatic instruction to pad them to 10,000.

### Evidence standard

All findings cite the specific GitHub chapter lines and distinguish confirmed contradictions from uncertain implications or stylistic recommendations. The reports may become stale when chapter text is revised; record updated blob SHA and re-run checks after merging.
