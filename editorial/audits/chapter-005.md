## Editorial verdict
**This chapter has serious character-identity contamination.** Much of Clarisse's supposed history duplicates Thalia's relationship with Annabeth, while Percy/Annabeth's established memories are explicitly contradicted. There are also clear sister-age/mother errors and a major location-of-records inconsistency. Preserve the concept of Percy confronting Poseidon's unequal parenting; re-establish **Clarisse's own** relationship with Silena and Percy.
