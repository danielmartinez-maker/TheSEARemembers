## Locked continuity constraints

- The only future-memory holders are Percy and Annabeth. They were romantically together in the previous life and remain an established, age-appropriate, nonsexual couple; never write a second first-courtship or disclose the rewrite to families accidentally.
- Percy, Clarisse and Silena are Poseidon's children and Cabin Three siblings; Percy is the youngest. They have distinct histories, reactions to royal Atlantis and relationships with Poseidon.
- Thalia, Annabeth and Jason are Zeus and Beryl's children. Thalia raised Annabeth; their disagreements about protection and autonomy cannot be resolved by one perfect speech. Jason's presence requires travel logistics.
- Zeus is absent, inconsistent and volatile, with a different appearance on each visit. Poseidon is not interchangeable with Zeus; Amphitrite is a parental figure without replacing Sally.
- Percy cannot recall his changed Atlantis childhood in the ordinary autobiographical way. Annabeth cannot suddenly possess those lost memories. No new alteration mechanism or secret time architect may be inferred from a casual scene.
- Do not backfill chapter 140+ ages into 91–120 automatically. Date/season and age need to be verified from the present chapter sequence; do not invent a calendar date to make a transition work.
- Camp's canoe lake is freshwater. The saltwater Sound and underwater Atlantis are distinct locations with different travel conditions.
- Write third-person limited, one focalizer per scene. No `POV:` headings in prose. Meaningful Percabeth and sibling beats should emerge from scene objectives, not ritualized reassurance.
- Chapter 090 ends after a divine storm, with Annabeth sleeping in Cabin Three as Thalia and the three Poseidon siblings settle. The shoreline recovery should still inform chapter 091.
- Chapter 120 ends with Amphitrite's invitation still awaiting a decision. Chapter 121 opens with Cabin Three negotiating independence, not with a visit already booked.

## Expansion briefs: distinct scene functions

### 091–100: deepen existing long chapters rather than restart them

**091 Tide Pools.** Preserve the approved excursion, family group and protected tide-pool ecosystem. Add a concrete problem caused by the previous storm (damaged access route or stranded small creatures) that requires different skills from each sibling; establish who supervises beyond the border and why; pay off the shared shell/sketch as specific later objects. Let Annabeth participate without erasing the PCS-only purpose of the outing.

**092 Jealousy II.** Give jealousy an observable trigger and a wrong decision by Percy that costs someone time or trust, not just an internal declaration of possessiveness. Distinguish Clarisse's pride from romantic exclusivity. Make the corrective action a chosen change in drill behavior or a repaired social plan; minimize summaries saying he has learned.

**093 Movie Night.** Put the projector/film arrangements under practical pressure and let campers solve problems according to their abilities. Build a movie-choice disagreement that reveals taste and belonging without escalating into a manufactured rupture. End with a small commitment that can echo in later group gatherings, not universal sleep in a perfect tableau.

**094 Sick Day.** Preserve Percy's illness and household care. Expand the medical sequence with credible monitoring, time passing, consequences for each sibling's plans and a clear limit on ambrosia/nectar as a treatment. Give Percy specific agency despite being sick; do not let fever become a substitute for private confessions.

**095 Annabeth Sick.** Avoid a beat-for-beat inversion of 094. Center Thalia's conflicted experience as a sister with competence but imperfect restraint, Annabeth's embarrassment at reliance on others, and Will's actual medical boundaries. Recovery should leave at least one deferred obligation or changed plan.

**096 Training Together.** Honor recovery time after Annabeth's fever; do not resume maximal storm exertion immediately without clearance. Stage one precisely described exercise with measurable failures, adaptations and feedback; let Percy support without magically absorbing the power cost. Preserve the final sea-and-storm partnership with concrete limitations.

**097 The Map.** Show Annabeth gathering source measurements, negotiating privacy/security for camp features and revising a design after disagreement. An attractive finished map is not enough; make an architectural trade-off visible. Establish the map's storage and controlled access for future chapters.

**098 Fathers' Day (Gods).** Clarisse, Silena and Percy should not have identical experiences of Poseidon. Give Thalia and Annabeth separate, credible reasons to engage or refuse Zeus. Avoid turning a divine visit into instant reconciliation; track Tyson and Triton's actual arrival/departure logistics and make any unequal attention consequential.

**099 Mothers.** Sally's death in the altered past is not Percy's remembered original loss, and Amphitrite's active parenting does not nullify it. Put these truths in friction during a concrete task (letter, remembrance, recipe or gift). Give the Grace sisters room for conflicting reactions to Beryl, not a generic parental tribute.

**100 The Thousandth Campfire.** Preserve the community-scale celebration and ceremonial details. Build an operational problem and a collective response instead of a succession of tributes; identify who keeps the campfire count and why a thousand is meaningful. If memories of Bob, Tartarus or the former timeline appear, keep them solely within the two memory holders unless another character has an independently verified reason to know.

### 101–110: these are currently short scene summaries, not full chapters

**101 A Name for It.** Shift from a label for established Percabeth to a concrete disagreement about how the couple is treated publicly. Include an ordinary shared activity, a Thalia/Annabeth boundary decision, and a behavioral outcome lasting into 102.

**102 Not a Competition.** Give Clarisse and Percy a real competitive activity whose rules expose status inequality and unequal training history. Allow Clarisse to be wrong in one respect and Percy in another; preserve a useful rivalry without closing all resentment.

**103 The Things We Remember.** Ground the letter in an actual writing and delivery process, with revision and privacy decisions. Contrast remembered events with facts verifiable in the altered present; do not invent new memory holders. Let each partner decline one question.

**104 A Day That Was Theirs.** Develop Annabeth's chosen date through logistics and competing duties; give her a concrete interest in the outing independent of Percy's mood. End with one newly established ritual or useful observation, not only an assurance that the day was theirs.

**105 The Place at the Table.** Turn the invitation into a scene about seating, belonging and expectations: whose place is fixed, who is visiting and what happens when the plan changes. Clarisse's gesture should be legible through action, not a sudden confession.

**106 The Visit That Wasn't a Test.** Expand Poseidon's visit around a specific family activity, miscommunication and a real redistribution of attention. His behavior should reveal parental limits as well as care, particularly Clarisse/Silena versus Percy's Atlantean childhood. Do not make the god solve everything by declaring equality.

**107 The Words We Choose.** Replace repetitive declarations of love with a situation in which the exact phrasing materially changes what someone agrees to, refuses, or misunderstands. Percabeth already know they love each other; the question is what they can honestly promise now.

**108 Three Things We Keep.** Use cabin rules and objects as memory-bearing practical constraints. Make the three chosen things cost space or require compromise, and follow a sibling who initially loses the argument before finding another way to belong.

**109 No Borrowed Promises.** Preserve the photograph and new sketch. Develop its provenance and careful handling in the altered world; the physical old-life evidence needs privacy and a verified explanation of why it survived. Do not force it into a new time-rewrite explanation.

**110 The Promise We Make Now.** The current draft jumps from making a promise to monthly repetition, spring revisions and 'that evening' without stable elapsed time. Fix the chronology explicitly or break the montage into a truly dated sequence. Give at least one failed promise and actual repair. Avoid another generic vows circle after 107/109; use a project with consequences.

### 111–114: full scene architecture required (all currently under 800 words)

**111 The Date Committee.** Preserve the meddling breakfast, Annabeth selecting the amphitheater, sketches, strawberries, and sisters being spotted. **112 Her Turn to Worry.** Clarify that Percy misses Atlantis, Amphitrite and Triton without suddenly recalling an overwritten childhood in detail. Stage a concrete incoming family request and show Annabeth's temptation to solve it for him, before she decides to let him speak on his own behalf. Leave the decision genuinely open for later Atlantis invitations.

**113 Little Brother.** Make Clarisse's illness distinct from 094/095, with an actual duty she refuses to relinquish and practical consequences. Percy contributes in a way that reflects his younger-brother role, Silena wants something for herself, and Annabeth's care remains friendship, not a substitute sibling tie. Show staged recovery rather than miraculous next-day combat.

**114 The Space Between.** Keep the disputed Atlantean song, the fact that Percy only heard it on an actual visit, and Annabeth's valid resistance to being tested. Develop the consequences of Percy's questioning and a meaningful independent Grace-sister scene. The lyrics' purported altered-childhood origins must remain secondhand, not remembered as a child by Percy.

### 115–120: revise existing later sequence, preserve the arc into 121

**115 The Old Photograph.** Preserve the physical photograph and shelf work. Audit who knows the photo's source and when; add a choice about display/privacy with real fallout. Avoid resolving earlier 109 photo handling by inventing a new explanation.

**116 Cabin Three Rules.** Make the rules useful in a concrete test: shared space, chores, personal property or privacy while guests arrive. Let siblings disagree about fairness and redistribute a burden. Keep the written agreement and track any rule carried forward.

**117 A Day Apart.** Annabeth and Percy should gain separate accomplishments, independent dialogue partners and an imperfect reunion that doesn't imply distrust. Avoid an omniscient account of both halves within one scene. Ensure each person's new knowledge survives into 118.

**118 A Message from Amphitrite.** Differentiate this earlier contact from the explicit invitation of 120. Track the message medium, contents and who heard it; clarify what Percy might know by testimony versus autobiographical recall. Make sibling reactions vary without coercing travel.

**119 Sisters' Errand.** Integrate storm clean-up, supply constraints and Grace sibling dynamics. Preserve the cabin decoration/chime and identify where it is hung for later continuity. Remove the extra trailing scene divider when line-editing and verify whether the weather relates to 090 or a separate event.

**120 A Letter from the Sea.** Keep the bottle, Amphitrite's seal, Clarisse/Silena's differing hesitations, Annabeth's independent choice and no immediate deadline. Build the invitation through competing family obligations and preparation discussions. Its ending must leave the visit undecided so 121's Cabin Three pact remains necessary.
