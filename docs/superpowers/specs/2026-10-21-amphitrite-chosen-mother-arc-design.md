# Amphitrite's Chosen-Mother Arc

**Status:** Awaiting author review of this written spec  
**Design approval:** The author approved the in-chat direction on 2026-10-21.  
**Source baseline:** `danielmartinez-maker/TheSEARemembers`, PR #103, head `3906567d3595d27fc318e8ad4d6cb99e8e581c6b`.

## Goal

Develop Amphitrite into a growing maternal figure for Silena and Clarisse, with each sister choosing to call her “Mom” after distinct, sustained relationship growth. Percy already treats Amphitrite as his mother; his spoken address should be “Mom.” The bonds are chosen and personal, not a replacement of the girls’ other mothers or a change to biological parentage.

## Source-grounded starting point

The current manuscript already establishes gradual, non-coercive groundwork:

- In Chapter 125, Amphitrite lets Silena and Clarisse choose their forms of address. Silena keeps “Your Majesty” for now, and Clarisse’s title remains undecided.
- In Chapter 128, Clarisse names the hurt of seeing Percy’s Atlantis childhood and Poseidon’s attention when she and Silena did not receive the same.
- In Chapter 129, Amphitrite hugs Silena and offers Clarisse a hand, which Clarisse accepts; neither gesture demands gratitude or a title.
- In Chapter 132, Silena says Amphitrite made the visit easier, while making clear that this did not rewrite Clarisse’s past.
- Chapter 193 is the current manuscript endpoint. Neither sister has called Amphitrite “Mom” in the chapters reviewed for this design.

These scenes remain the foundation. The sisters’ titles are future milestones, not retroactive facts.

## Relationship progression

### Percy

Use “Mom” as Percy’s direct form of address for Amphitrite, including the existing spoken address in Chapter 99 and the family reference in Chapter 132. Keep “mother” as an ordinary descriptive word where grammatically appropriate; do not run a global replacement. Sally remains Percy’s mother and his grief for her remains present. Amphitrite is still unaware of Percy’s erased-future memories and altered-childhood memory gap.

### Silena

Silena reaches “Mom” first, in a quiet, personal moment after Amphitrite has shown sustained interest in Silena’s own work, choices, and limits—not only in her connection to Percy. Let the word arrive naturally without a public announcement, demand, or response that makes Silena responsible for Amphitrite’s feelings. Silena’s history with Maria remains hers and is not erased or solved by this bond.

### Clarisse

Clarisse reaches “Mom” later, after repeated care that respects her anger and grief about Poseidon’s unequal involvement and recognizes Clarisse as her own person. One gift, crisis, apology, or Atlantis visit cannot earn the title by itself. Clarisse’s choice does not forgive Poseidon, obligate her to visit Atlantis, or replace her own mother. Amphitrite does not ask Clarisse to use the title.

The sisters reach the milestone in separate scenes and at different paces. Their growth can include setbacks or continued use of Amphitrite’s name or title without undoing trust already earned.

## Scope

- Amend `FIC_CONTEXT.md` with the approved relationship direction and title boundaries.
- Update Percy, Silena, and Clarisse’s continuity profiles so they distinguish current source facts from planned future milestones.
- Change Percy’s direct spoken address to “Mom” in the source passages where “Mother” is currently his chosen address, including Chapters 99 and 132; inspect other Percy/Amphitrite address candidates before editing.
- Keep Chapters 125–132’s existing sister choices and hurt intact; only revise their prose if a source-specific continuity need is identified.
- Record the sisters’ “Mom” milestones as future story work after Chapter 193. This spec does not draft a new full chapter or claim either milestone has occurred.

## Boundaries and non-goals

Do not change biological parentage, overwrite Silena’s or Clarisse’s relationships with their own mothers, resolve Poseidon’s past through Amphitrite’s care, or imply that a chosen title transfers memories or grants Amphitrite knowledge of Percy’s secret. Do not make the sisters call Amphitrite “Mom” together or before the relationship earns it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The canon bible records the gradual, voluntary arc and distinguishes the three daughters’ relationships to Amphitrite.
2. In Percy’s dialogue, he addresses her as “Mom” and uses “Mom” when speaking informally about her; narration and other characters’ formal or descriptive references to Amphitrite remain context-sensitive.
3. Existing Chapters 125–132 retain the sisters’ autonomy and Clarisse’s unresolved grievance.
4. The continuity profiles mark Silena’s earlier milestone and Clarisse’s later milestone as planned, not as events already in the source.
5. No prose claims that either sister has called Amphitrite “Mom” by Chapter 193.
6. Future chapter work shows distinct, earned scenes for each sister before their title milestones; whole-story completion is not claimed until those scenes exist.
