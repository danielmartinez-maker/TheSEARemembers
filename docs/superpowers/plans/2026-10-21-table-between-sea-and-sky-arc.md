- Percy and Annabeth alone retain erased-future autobiographical memory. Amphitrite does not know Percy’s secret. The architect stays unknown.
- Percabeth stays established, younger, and nonsexual. No love triangle or romantic subplot for either new sibling.
- Carry forward the existing approved chosen-mother thread: Silena chooses “Mom” privately and before Clarisse; Clarisse’s choice comes later, in a separate earned scene. Neither title replaces a biological mother or forgives Poseidon.
- Every chapter is limited POV, has distinct movement, warm centerpiece, Percabeth beat, and sibling interaction; do not make these formula scenes.
- Every newly drafted full chapter has at least 10,000 body words. Target 10,000–10,200 unless a scene earns more; cumulative minimum 250,000. Count consistently after writing.
- Avoid padded writing. Search every-offset 300-word windows after the full run; repair later occurrences only.
